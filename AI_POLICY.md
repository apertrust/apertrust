# AI policy

Apertrust is built with heavy AI assistance, its darktable ports above all; the [README](README.md)'s note says why. This page says how AI-assisted contributions are treated.

- **Welcome, under the same rules.** Contributions are welcome whether or not AI tools were used. Every contribution is treated the same and judged by the project's rules in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **You are accountable.** You are responsible for every line you submit. You understand it, and you can explain and defend it in review.
- **Small and scoped.** Changes stay small and scoped to an issue.
- **Your own words.** A pull request's description is yours: written by you, or checked by you before you post it.
- **A human in the loop.** Autonomous agents do not open issues, review or comment in the project's spaces without a person who directs them and answers for what they post.
- **Worth its review.** A contribution whose review would cost more than it is worth may be closed with a standard reply. LLVM's [AI tool policy](https://llvm.org/docs/AIToolPolicy.html#extractive-contributions) calls these extractive contributions.
- **Licences.** Generated code must not reproduce code under a licence incompatible with the GPL. Ports keep darktable's provenance and copyright lines.
- **Disclosure is optional.** AI help is disclosed once, for the whole repository, here and in the README. An `Assisted-by:` or `Co-Authored-By:` trailer is allowed but never required. Every commit still carries a person's `Signed-off-by:`.

The agent kit, [apertrust/agent-kit](https://github.com/apertrust/agent-kit), helps AI-assisted contributors follow the project's rules.
