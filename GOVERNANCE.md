# Governance

## Today

Apertrust has one maintainer, Andrii Ryzhkov, who decides for now: what is planned, what is merged and who may join.

Design decisions are recorded in [docs/decisions/](docs/decisions/README.md), each with its options and the reason for the choice. A recorded decision is settled; changing it is a new decision, proposed in an issue.

## How roles may grow

As people contribute, roles can be added:

- **Reviewers** review pull requests in an area they know. They are invited after a record of good contributions or reviews there.
- **Maintainers** can merge and share the decisions. They are invited from the reviewers.

New roles and the people in them will be listed here.

## Disagreements

Disagreements are settled in the open, in the issue or the pull request: each side states its case, with evidence where it can, such as a measurement or a golden result. The maintainer decides when there is no agreement, and records the decision and its reason. Everyone follows the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
