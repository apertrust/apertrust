# 0109. A style on every selected photo: one batch, one Undo

## Context

[0106](0106-presets-and-styles-as-appreset-and-apstyle-files.md) applies a style to the active photo as one change, `ApplyStyle`, and plans it on every selected photo as one batch with one Undo (#373). [0037](0037-one-view-the-photos-tab.md) makes such an action act on every selected photo, the count shown, one Undo for all, and plans an edit to a photo that is not live as a change of its edit and sidecar without the GPU. [history.md](../history.md) plans the batch's undo: taken back on every photo where its last step is still the end, a photo changed since keeping it under its own steps. Left open: the command's shape, how a photo that is not open is changed, the batch's id, clock and source across photos, Undo across photos while the app runs and after a restart, photos that cannot take the style, what the person sees, and what a cancel leaves. Apply Edit on the selection is #372's to decide.

**darktable at the reference release** applies styles to the selected images in two parts (`dt_control_apply_styles`, `src/control/jobs/control_jobs.c:2493-2522`, through `_add_history_job_data`, `:2360-2411`). The image open in the darkroom is taken out of the list and run by itself as a synchronous job (`:2378-2392`), which `dt_control_add_job` runs at once on the calling thread (`src/control/jobs.c:396-405`); the rest go to a `DT_JOB_QUEUE_USER_FG` job with a progress (`:2396-2402`). Each run of `_control_apply_styles_job_run` (`:1763-1828`) applies image by image until cancelled inside an undo group of its own, `DT_UNDO_LT_HISTORY`, opened at `:1781` and closed at `:1818`: so a selection holding the darkroom image is two undo groups, and two Ctrl+Z take it all back. Its undo lives in memory and is gone at quit; its progress is `PROGRESS_BLOCKING`, holding the person's other interactions but cancel.

**What constrains it:** one live photo at a time ([0037](0037-one-view-the-photos-tab.md)); the edit's rules in the engine's `edit` and `history`, which `apertrust-cli edit` already applies to a sidecar outside any session ([0078](0078-undo-redo-and-the-edits-history.md)); the image context the session takes from the raw's metadata before the decode ([0077](0077-a-photo-opens-in-stages.md)), which `apertrust-raw`'s corpus test holds equal to the decode's, field for field but the pixels, for all 40 samples; the jobs and their rows ([0057](0057-jobs-as-darktables-control-jobs.md), [0072](0072-each-job-reports-its-own-row.md)); and the edit writer with its check before each write ([0078](0078-undo-redo-and-the-edits-history.md), [0052](0052-comparing-two-copies-of-a-sidecar.md)).

## Decision

**A style on several photos is one `ApplyStyle` naming them: the active photo changed at once, as built, and the others by one job that reads each one's metadata and sidecar, applies the style's rules to its log with no decode and no GPU, and writes it over the file as read; every photo's steps share one batch id, which one Undo takes back on every photo where it is still the last.** Planned, as #373's steps ([history.md](../history.md#the-plan)), following darktable's job above but for its two undo groups and its blocking progress.

### The command

- **`ApplyStyle` gains `photos: Vec<PathBuf>`.** Empty, it is the active photo alone, as built, refused as now (`NotLive`, `ViewOnly`, a value refused). Otherwise the photos named, each by its path made absolute as `Selection::photos` names it, any path as `Export`'s are, each once, in the order given. The app sends the selection's photos when more than one is selected, else none. [0037](0037-one-view-the-photos-tab.md) plans commands naming photos by id; until then a style names them by path, as `Export` does, and changes with it.
- **One batch:** the session draws its `BatchId` and reads the wall clock once, as the command is applied.
- **The active photo,** where `photos` holds it, is changed at once in the command's own turn by the path built for it: its steps, `Values`, `History` and frame in that turn, an undo record on its own stack, the batch's id on its steps. The command still ends a gesture under way and is refused whole while the active photo opens (`NotLive`), as any command on the edit, whether or not `photos` holds it; the Styles tool's rows are disabled then.
- **The other photos are one job** ([The job](#the-job)). With only the active photo to change there is no job.
- **With `photos` not empty, a photo that cannot take the style is skipped and named, never refusing the command,** the active photo among them: open for viewing only, or refusing a value of the style (`NotListed`), it is skipped and named as any other ([Skipped photos](#skipped-photos)). So the Styles tool's rows stay enabled with several photos selected though the active photo is view-only.
- **`ClearStyle` keeps no photos:** Clear acts on the active photo alone. The Styles tool's list of styles applied is the active photo's (`Values::styles`), so Clear on the selection would change lists no one sees; Reset acts on the active photo alone too ([0037](0037-one-view-the-photos-tab.md)); and right after applying, the batch's Undo takes the style back from every photo. `ClearStyle` can gain `photos` the same way later.

### A photo that is not open

For each photo but the active one, the job's body, on its worker:

1. **On the disk:** a raw that is not, a cloud placeholder (`listing::is_local`), is skipped, as a thumbnail job reads none.
2. **The image context:** the raw's metadata without its pixels (`RawFile::metadata`, rawler's decode with `dummy`) made `raw::image_context`, as the open's stage 2 makes it; a raw that cannot be read, or is no 16-bit mosaic, is skipped.
3. **The sidecar:** read through the edit writer once every write handed over before is done (`Writer::read`, as an export's snapshot is), with its **stamp**: its size, its modified time and the BLAKE3 hash of the bytes read, as `apertrust-cli edit` stamps a file, so a write that kept the size and set the time back is still seen. It is read with the tool definitions (`sidecar::from_xml_at`); a newer build's edit, or one with a `sidecar::Problem`, is skipped as view-only; no sidecar is a photo with no history. **A log that already holds the command's batch id** is skipped too, unchanged and counted as done: the batch reached it already, as by the order below.
4. **The style's rules on its log,** by the same function the session uses for the active photo (`history`), so the two cannot differ:
   - the fold clamped as the open's stage 2 clamps it (`Edit::clamp_read`), so a clamp is no step, as on an opened photo;
   - a photo with no history given its default steps first (`Log::add_defaults`), as an opened photo's first change and `apertrust-cli edit` give them; the end among them, the first change's rule; the end below the last step, the later steps dropped, which no Undo brings back on a photo not open, as with `edit`;
   - `Reset` first for Replace, its Reset step always recorded; then each tool's values as `SetTool` sets them on that photo's image context; a value the photo refuses (`NotListed`, an option it does not list) skips the photo;
   - a step of each tool it changes, source Style, noted `history-style` with the style's name, each with the batch's id, a change of one step included; the list of styles applied on the last step by decision 106's rules, applied again keeping each tool's earlier before state;
   - each step's clock taken at the command's wall time after the photo's latest clock as `history::latest` reads it, its log's and its copy's vector observed in turn so another machine's clock past the drift bound is ignored (`clock::MAX_DRIFT`, [0047](0047-where-edits-live-and-what-the-sidecar-holds.md)); the copy's clock vector advanced.
5. **The file's XML,** built on the worker, handed to the engine thread, which alone knows which photo is open:
   - **open and live,** a photo opened since the command: the worker's result is dropped and the style applied to the photo as to the active one, a gesture under way ended first;
   - **opening:** the result is held until the photo is live, then dropped and applied as above, or until its open ends, then the body starts again from step 3; a cancel of the job drops a result held so, the photo unchanged;
   - **any other:** handed to the edit writer with the stamp step 3 read.
6. **The write** is the safe write, made only over the file as read: the writer compares the file's stamp with the one read and, where it differs, writes nothing and the body starts again from step 3, twice more, then names the photo as changing, as the session's `StillChanging`. **The writer's generation is kept by file:** a newer file taken in, or another photo opened, drops only the writes of its own file handed over before, so a write for a photo that is not live is never dropped by another file's generation, and only its own stamp stops it. A write that fails is named, the job ending failed with the first such failure ([0072](0072-each-job-reports-its-own-row.md)).

**In the order sent, the active photo too.** A command that changes the active photo while an earlier batch job still has that photo to come first has the engine thread apply that batch to it, as to the active photo, an undo record of its own, then the command; the job, reaching the photo, finds the batch's id in its log and skips it (step 3). So with Warm film running over 200 photos, a person who opens one not yet reached and applies Cool film there gets Warm film first and Cool film over it, as on every other photo where both land.

**What `SetTool`'s checks read of the photo,** all from the camera's metadata: the options a choice lists (`ProcessingTool::options`: Exposure's, Demosaic's, the input colour profile's); the defaults for the photo, which `Edit::clamp_read` folds into every value before it clamps (`ProcessingTool::defaults`: Colour calibration's, Exposure's, White balance's and others); and White balance's redraw (`ProcessingTool::redrawn`). A range that follows other controls reads the values alone (`ProcessingTool::range`). The pixels, the raw's histogram, the output profile and the full-resolution image are `prepare`'s alone. **So nothing is skipped, refused or deferred for any kind of tool,** the `in_styles` tools or any other a style holds: a photo is skipped only whole. Where a file's decode would give another image context, its open clamps it again, as for any opened edit ([engine.md](../engine.md#opening-a-photo)).

**Cost:** the metadata read is most of each photo's time, 38 to 44 ms for the 61 MP Sony 7R V on the M4's SSD ([engine.md](../engine.md#opening-a-photo)), so 200 such photos take about 8 to 9 s on one worker; over SMB it is to be measured. Should that be too slow, a definition's flag can skip the read where no tool of the style reads the photo.

### The job

- **Kind `apply-style`, on `UserForeground`,** as [0057](0057-jobs-as-darktables-control-jobs.md) places styles, cancellable, its progress the photos to change but the active one: title "Applying "Warm film" to 12 photos", detail "3 of 11 done · now IMG_2044.ARW", outcome "Warm film applied to 11 of 12 photos", each a message id with its arguments ([0072](0072-each-job-reports-its-own-row.md)). It offers no retry.
- **The jobs window never opens itself for it,** nor for its `undo-batch`: the Toolbar's jobs spinner shows it runs and the notice tells how it ended ([What the person sees](#what-the-person-sees-and-the-events)), and the person can still open the window for its progress and its Cancel. Its `Added` says so, `quiet` ([0072](0072-each-job-reports-its-own-row.md)), so a client needs no code for its kind. An exception to the window opening for every job the person starts.
- **One at a time,** in the order sent, the next batch job waiting for the one before, so a photo's batches land in the order their commands were sent.
- **Cancel** is looked for between photos; a write already handed over completes, a result held for a photo still opening is dropped. The photos done keep the batch, the rest are untouched, and one Undo takes back those done.
- **Editing never waits on it:** unlike darktable's blocking progress, the person goes on editing the active photo ([0057](0057-jobs-as-darktables-control-jobs.md)). Quitting asks first, as for any job the person started.

### One batch across photos

- **One `BatchId`,** the session's, drawn once per command and written into every step of every photo it changes, a change of one step included, where a change of one tool on one photo otherwise has none. It is checked against the active photo's log as now, not against the others': 64 random bits make a clash with any of 1,000 batches in one log about 1 in 10^16, and a log that holds it already is skipped (step 3).
- **One source and note,** Style and `history-style` with the style's name, on every photo.
- **One time:** each photo's clock is the wall time read as the command is applied, after that photo's latest clock, so the History tab shows one time on every photo unless a photo's clocks were ahead of it, as another computer's can be within the drift bound ([0047](0047-where-edits-live-and-what-the-sidecar-holds.md)).
- **One Undo,** where darktable's selection holding the darkroom image is two undo groups: the active photo's change and the job's are one batch, on purpose, so the count the notice gives is what one Undo takes back.
- **A built-in style is matched by its file:** the list's rules match a person's style by name ([0106](0106-presets-and-styles-as-appreset-and-apstyle-files.md#the-styles-applied-to-a-photo)), and the client knows only the active photo's list, so the engine matches by file where either the item's or the applied style's file is a `builtin:<id>`, the two then the same style only where both files are the same, and by name only where neither is; a built-in matched takes the name sent. A built-in applied in another language on one of the photos moves to the top rather than being listed twice, and a person's style named as a built-in is never taken for it. `ClearStyle` names the item's file too, `ClearStyle { name, file }`, so Clear on a built-in's row of a built-in and a person's style of one name takes the built-in alone.

### Undo across photos

- **The session remembers each batch it applied on several photos:** its id, the style's name and the photos it changed, the latest 64 for its life, as it keeps the retries ([0072](0072-each-job-reports-its-own-row.md)).
- **`Undo`** on the active photo, where its move would take back steps of a remembered batch, by a record or by the fallback, does so there, then on each other photo of the batch whose end is still on the batch's last step: a job of kind `undo-batch` on `UserForeground`, cancellable, which moves that end back before the batch's first step as Undo's fallback does, the steps kept after the end, the copy's clock vector advanced. Each photo is routed as the `apply-style` job routes it: **open and live,** Undo there, as its own Undo would take the batch back; **opening,** held until it is live; **any other,** through the writer, over the file as read, by its stamp. A photo whose end has moved keeps the batch under its own steps and is named as changed since; there its own Undo takes its own steps first, then the batch on it alone. A cancel leaves the photos done taken back; another Undo of the batch goes on with the rest.
- **A batch whose `apply-style` job still runs** is cancelled first, then taken back on the photos it reached.
- **`Redo`** puts a remembered batch back the same way, on each photo whose next step after the end is the batch's first.
- **`UndoBatch { batch }`,** the notice's Undo, from any photo, active or not, does on each of the batch's photos what Undo would do there for that batch: on the active photo, an Undo where its Undo would take the batch back, else it has changed since; on the others, as above. A batch the session does not remember is refused, `UnknownBatch`. It is a history command: from an agent's connection it is refused unless the batch is that agent's own, as its `Undo` is ([0108](0108-agents-through-apertrust-mcp-joining-the-apps-session.md)).
- **`History`'s `undo` and `redo`:** `Move::Steps` gains `photos`, the remembered batch's count of photos, else 1, so Edit › Undo names it ("Undo Warm film on 12 Photos"), as every batch item names its count ([gui.md](../gui.md#selection-and-the-active-photo)).
- **After a restart** the session's memory is gone, while each photo's sidecar keeps the batch id on its steps: Undo on a photo takes the batch back on that photo alone, through its fallback, as darktable's undo group is gone at quit. Planned after #373, with no change to the file: the collection's listing reads each photo's last applied step's batch id, so Undo after a restart finds the batch's other photos still on it.

### Skipped photos

A photo is skipped, unchanged and named, when its raw is not on the disk; its raw cannot be read or is no 16-bit mosaic; its edit is open for viewing only, a newer Apertrust's or one with a `sidecar::Problem`, with its reason; a value of the style is one it refuses; or its file kept changing. A photo already holding the batch is skipped as done, not named. A skip is not a failure: the job ends finished, its outcome counting the photos changed ("applied to 11 of 12 photos").

### What the person sees, and the events

- **`Event::Batch(BatchEvent)`,** notices (planned; [events.md](../events.md)), naming the style's batch as `batch: BatchId`, apart from the engine's batches of commands, which a client knows only by their numbers:
  - `Changed { batch, photo, edit }`, as each photo's write lands, the active photo's in the command's turn, `edit` its `EditState` as classified after the write, so a client marks the Browser's pencil and asks for its thumbnail again;
  - `Ended { command, batch, action, style, changed, skipped, cancelled }`, `command` the number of the command that started it, `action` `Applied`, `TakenBack` or `PutBack`, `changed` the photos changed and `skipped` each skipped photo with its reason, those changed since among them for a take-back; with no job, in the command's turn.
- **The active photo** changes at once; the others' pencils as their writes land. Their thumbnails are asked for again: a photo that is not live is rendered once the library's step 6 renders other photos, and until then keeps its picture ([library.md](../library.md#thumbnails)).
- **The notice** over the photo, as the app shows a notice that is no job's: "Warm film applied to 12 photos · Undo"; with photos skipped, "Warm film applied to 11 of 12 photos; IMG_2045 is view-only · Undo"; after an Undo, "Warm film taken back on 11 of 12 photos; IMG_2045 has changed since". Its Undo sends `UndoBatch`.
- **The Styles tool,** with several photos selected, says so in its rows' tooltips ("Apply to 12 photos"); Clear stays the active photo's.

## Options considered

- **`Step` gaining photos:** `ApplyStyle` is no command a `Step` holds, and keeps the list's rules in the session ([0106](0106-presets-and-styles-as-appreset-and-apstyle-files.md)). Apply Edit's command is #372's to decide; it may use this job and this Undo.
- **A separate command, `ApplyStyleTo { photos, … }`:** two commands of the same fields and rules; an empty `photos` keeps today's meaning, so no client changes.
- **Acting on the session's selection, naming no photos:** another client may change the selection between the person's look and the command, so the count shown and the photos changed could differ; `Export` names its photos too.
- **`ClearStyle` with photos:** above.
- **The whole command refused where the active photo is view-only or refuses a value:** the person who selected 12 photos would get nothing for one; with `photos` named, every photo is judged alike.
- **Opening each photo, decode and GPU, to apply it:** about 0.5 s and a 61 MP decode a photo, against [0037](0037-one-view-the-photos-tab.md)'s edit without the GPU, when only the metadata is read.
- **No image context, as `apertrust-cli edit` sets a value other than a choice:** clamps and options would differ from the opened photo's, so one style would give two edits on one photo, opened or not.
- **The worker writing each file itself, as `apertrust-cli edit` does:** a photo opened meanwhile could read its file before the write and show its edit without the style until its next change; the engine thread knows which photo is open, so it routes each write.
- **The whole change on the engine thread:** the XML of a 1,000-step log takes 6 to 16 ms on the M4 ([history.md](../history.md#in-the-sidecar)), per photo; the worker builds it, and the engine thread only routes.
- **The writer's one generation for every file, as built:** taking in the live photo's newer file, or opening another, would drop a batch's writes of other files handed over before it, silently.
- **A stamp of size and modified time alone, as the live photo's:** a program that keeps the size and sets the time back would be written over; the CLI hashes for that reason.
- **A later command on the active photo before an earlier batch reaches it:** Cool film then Warm film on one photo and Warm film then Cool film on the others, from the same two clicks.
- **One clock for every photo:** a photo whose clocks are ahead, another computer's, would get a step older than its last, against the hybrid clock's rule ([0047](0047-where-edits-live-and-what-the-sidecar-holds.md)).
- **A batch id for each photo:** Undo could not find the batch across photos; one command is one batch ([0078](0078-undo-redo-and-the-edits-history.md)).
- **darktable's two undo groups, the darkroom image's and the job's:** two Undos for one click, and a notice whose count one Undo does not match.
- **Undo taking the batch back on every photo whatever came after,** as darktable's lighttable undo puts each image's history back: it would throw away a later edit of a photo; [history.md](../history.md) keeps the batch under it.
- **The batch's photos kept in a file for Undo after a restart:** a second truth beside the sidecars, which hold the batch id already.
- **Reading a raw that is not on the disk:** a batch over a cloud folder would download every raw.
- **darktable's blocking progress:** no edit waits on a job ([0057](0057-jobs-as-darktables-control-jobs.md)).
- **Several batch jobs at once:** a photo's batches could land out of order, and the later one's Undo would find the earlier over it.
- **The jobs window opening for it as for every job the person starts** ([0072](0072-each-job-reports-its-own-row.md)): a dozen photos take about half a second, so the window would open and close again for each style applied, while the notice already tells the end.
- **The window opening only for a batch still running after 1 s:** a timing rule of its own, and the window over the photo just as the notice is; the spinner and a click give the same row and Cancel.

## Consequences

- **`apertrust-engine`'s `history`:** `Log::add_change` takes a batch the caller gives, and the session's `PhotoHistory::complete` the command's wall time, since it drew `Clock::now` itself; a style applied to a log, a pure function the session's active photo and the job share, tested to give the same log on a photo opened or not; a built-in's item matched by its `builtin:<id>` file.
- **Its session:** `ApplyStyle`'s `photos`; the `apply-style` and `undo-batch` jobs, `quiet`, one batch job at a time; a pending batch applied to the active photo before a later command; the writer's generation by file and its writes of a photo that is not live over the file as read, by size, modified time and hash; the remembered batches; `UndoBatch` and `SessionError::UnknownBatch`; `Move::Steps::photos`; `Event::Batch`.
- **`apertrust-base`'s `jobs`:** `Added` gains `quiet`, which a client reads to leave its jobs window closed ([0072](0072-each-job-reports-its-own-row.md)).
- **The app:** the Styles tool applying to the selection, its rows' tooltips, the notice and its Undo, the Browser's pencil from `Changed`, Edit › Undo naming the count; the two job kinds' icons in the design system's table.
- **The docs:** [history.md](../history.md#a-batch-on-several-photos), [session-protocol.md](../session-protocol.md#commands-planned), [events.md](../events.md), [engine.md](../engine.md#writing-an-edit-without-opening-the-photo), [gui.md](../gui.md#selection-and-the-active-photo), [library.md](../library.md#version-2), [0072](0072-each-job-reports-its-own-row.md), [0106](0106-presets-and-styles-as-appreset-and-apstyle-files.md) and the [roadmap](../roadmap.md).
- **Later:** Undo after a restart across the collection; the other photos' thumbnails rendered (the library's step 6); Apply Edit on the selection, whose command #372 decides; `ClearStyle` on several photos, if asked.
