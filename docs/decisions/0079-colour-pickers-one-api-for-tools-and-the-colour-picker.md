# 0079. Colour pickers, one API for tools and the Colour picker

## Context

darktable's modules pick values from the photo: Exposure, White balance, Colour calibration, Colour balance RGB, AgX, RGB curve and RGB levels in the first pass, Filmic RGB, the colour equalizer and blending later. Its color picker utility shows the colour under a point or an area and keeps samples to compare. Apertrust's definitions already hold the pickers' quads, drawn nowhere ([0029](0029-a-tool-is-darktables-page.md)), and [0019](0019-scopes-and-readouts-counted-on-the-gpu.md) left a readout over an area open. How are pickers turned on and moved, where and how is the photo measured, what does a tool do with a reading, and how do the button, the marks on the photo and the utility look?

darktable 5.6.2, the reference ([0002](0002-rewrite-darktable-first-then-apertrusts-own-way.md)), keeps one active picker, a module's or the utility's (`src/gui/color_picker_proxy.c`); measures the focused module's input and, with `DT_COLOR_PICKER_IO`, its output in the preview pipe, per channel mean, minimum and maximum in the module's colour space, the mosaic per CFA colour, optionally denoised (`src/common/color_picker.c`, `src/develop/pixelpipe_hb.c:1182-1198`, `:1568-1588`); applies a reading only when the area moved or the picker started (`color_picker_proxy.c:76-104`, `:288-328`); and draws and drags the point and area in the darkroom (`src/views/darkroom.c:331-470`, `:3815-4140`).

## Decision

**One active picker per session, a tool's or the Colour picker's, measured on the GPU at its tool's place in the live viewport pipe, its reading applied by the session through the tool's own pure function as one change of source Auto.** The design in full, with its types, darktable's behaviour cited line by line, the component, the tool, the tests and the plan, is [pickers.md](../pickers.md). Built: 79.1, the Colour picker's own picker through the session, `Command::Picker` with `On`, `Point`, `Area`, `Switch`, `Reset` and `Off` and `Event::Picker` with its frame part, its region kept through the geometry, measured on the frame by the engine's reduction and read back through the staging pair the scopes share; and 79.2, `apertrust-tools`'s side, `ProcessingDefinition::pickers` with their checks, a slider's picker and a line of pickers (`End::Picker(id)`, [0107](0107-packed-lines-one-line-row-with-end-items.md)) and `ProcessingTool::pick`, Exposure's, Colour balance RGB's and AgX's sliders naming their pickers; 79.3, the engine's side of tool pickers, the reduction at an operation's input and output in the render's encoder or a sample-only render, the mosaic per CFA colour and denoise, a reading applied through the tool's `pick`, a drag's one change, within the moved area's budgets on the M4; 79.4, the design system's on-photo overlay primitives; 79.5, `PickerButton` and the picker's marks; 79.6, the app's side, the quads and rows of pickers drawn with their state, the marks over the photo, the viewer bar's words, the keys and focus; 79.7, Exposure's picker, its settings in the settings schema; 79.8, White balance's, turned on by Settings' from image area; 79.9, Colour calibration's, on its readout row, its area colour mapping live; 79.10, Colour balance RGB's six, on its hues and fulcrums; and 79.17's engine side, the one-shot pick, `PickerCommand::Once` and `Region::pressed`. The rest is planned, in Phase 2: AgX's, RGB curve's and RGB levels' pickers, stored samples, the scopes restricted to the selection, and the Colour picker tool.

- **The protocol:** `Command::Picker(PickerCommand)` (`On { picker, area, keys, settings, channel }`, `Point`, `Area` with `done`, `Switch`, `Reset`, `Settings`, `Off`, and `Once`) and `Command::Samples(SamplesCommand)` (`Add`, `Lock`, `Load`, `Store`, `Remove`, `Clear`), view state, not commands on the edit; a picker that takes one kind kept to it, its `Switch`, and a `Point` or `Area` of the other kind, refused (`OneKind`, planned; [0105](0105-picker-gestures-a-click-within-6-points-the-pickers-own-kinds.md)); regions in the photo's coordinates, 0 to 1 across the photo as export renders it, kept by the session in the original image's coordinates through each geometry's maps ([0016](0016-geometry-for-operations-that-move-pixels.md)), as darktable keeps them. Two facts: `Event::Picker { through, reading }`, the active picker's region as drawn, its tool's input and output `Stats` (mean, min and max of four channels) and what its pick shows, and the frame's RGB and Lab under it, or a `Picker` with no reading as it turns off, its `through` every command applied then; `Event::Samples`, every stored sample's.
- **A one-shot pick** (built, 79.17): `PickerCommand::Once { picker, region, keys, settings, channel }`, a tool's picker on for one reading of `region`, its pick applied and the picker off again, one command for scripts, the MCP and assistants; the app's picker cursors turn the picker on with `On` instead (104.6; [0104](0104-cursors-one-chosen-at-a-time-in-a-well-on-the-toolbar.md)). A `Region::Area` is read as sent. A `Region::Point` is read as a plain click there reads in darktable: the point for a point picker and one with both kinds; for an area picker the area a press on the photo starts, 2 % of the photo's width each way and as tall in its pixels, clamped (`darkroom.c:4064-4073`), made by one function, `Region::pressed`, which the app's press on the photo makes its area with as built; planned, the app's press keeps that area's size at Fit on screen at every zoom, so the two agree at Fit ([0105](0105-picker-gestures-a-click-within-6-points-the-pickers-own-kinds.md)). The region is mapped through the geometry and measured in the fitted viewport pipe as every picker's is, at every zoom ([0099](0099-the-zoomed-view-a-detail-pipeline-beside-the-fitted-one.md)), with no frame part. Its tool is turned on where needed, as `On` turns it on, and the turning on and the pick's writes are one completed change of source Auto, one step, or, planned, from an agent of source Assistant with the request's note, refused as `PickerInUse` while the person's picker is on ([0108](0108-agents-through-apertrust-mcp-joining-the-apps-session.md)), the turning on held until the pick is written or found to write nothing, or a command on the edit comes first; no step where neither changes anything; no target, so two never merge. Then it is off, the picker's own point and area untouched. Its events are a `Picker` with its reading, the change's `Values`, `History` and `Frame`, then a `Picker` with none, in one batch; it is answered by a `Saved` as `On` is and refused with `On`'s reasons, `NotLive`, `ViewOnly`, `Hidden`, `UnknownPicker` (the Colour picker's among them), `UnknownSetting` and `NotReady`; it turns a picker on off first ([pickers.md](../pickers.md#a-one-shot-pick)).
- **Only one picker on at a time,** as darktable's `picker_proxy`. Stored samples, darktable's "live samples", coexist with it, each measured on every frame unless locked. Turning a tool's picker on turns its tool on, as darktable's does; the session turns it off on `Off` or another `On`, another photo, any change to its tool but its own picks, unless it is darktable's "keep-active", and its tool turning off; with no focused tool in Apertrust, the client turns it off when another tool's control changes, when another tool's Show on photo turns on (planned, with Rotate and perspective's port; one tool's on at a time) or when its own tool's section is collapsed in the Tools panel, whichever door turned it on ([0104](0104-cursors-one-chosen-at-a-time-in-a-well-on-the-toolbar.md)).
- **Measured on the GPU:** the engine's own reduction kernel, `crates/engine/slang/picker.slang`, no operation's pass, pairwise sums in a fixed order, darktable's B-spline denoise, its spaces (the operation's own, LCh, HSL, JzCzhz), several boxes a dispatch. A tool picker's runs right after its operation's last pass in the render's encoder while its key changes; a moved area with no edit runs a sample-only render from the newest cache point at or before the operation, the picker's operation being the edited instance, so after the downscale an input reading reads the edit cache directly. The frame's samples run in a submission of their own after the frame's copy and the scopes' count, on the fitted frame or, in a zoomed view, the fitted pipeline's frame, `Frame::overview`, which the scopes count then (decision 99). Read back through the scopes' and readouts' staging pair, never waited for; a failure turns pickers off, never the frame.
- **The scale is the fitted viewport's:** the viewport's size after the downscale, full resolution in the raw stage, never a zoomed view's region: with the zoomed view (planned), the fitted pipeline keeps running beside the detail, and the frame samples read its frame, `Frame::overview` ([0099](0099-the-zoomed-view-a-detail-pipeline-beside-the-fitted-one.md)); darktable's preview pipe's place under [0063](0063-departures-from-darktable-and-views-below-full-scale.md). Exports, stills, tiles, golden cases and derivatives never measure.
- **The tool side:** `ProcessingDefinition::pickers`, each a `Picker { id, operation, kind, denoise, output, space, keep, writes, settings }` from darktable's flags, with `words_by` and `ProcessingTool::picker_ready` ([0088](0088-exposures-page-as-redesigned.md)); a `Picker` end item (`End::Picker(id)`, [0107](0107-packed-lines-one-line-row-with-end-items.md)) at a slider's end and on a line row for darktable's picker buttons, and planned on a choice row; `ProcessingTool::pick`, pure, from a reading, the values, the image context, the tool's settings, the channel the tool shows, the tool's last reading and the keys held to the controls it writes and what its panel shows. The session applies the first reading after `On`, each reading after a move, and, after a change of the tool's `Settings` that includes one the picker of its latest reading reads, that stored reading again, not measured anew, as darktable's settings callbacks apply `picked_color` (`exposure.c:1171-1175`), nothing after any other change of them, darktable's rule, never one an upstream edit caused; a drag's readings are one gesture, its target the picker ([0078](0078-undo-redo-and-the-edits-history.md)). A picked value is stored as darktable stores it, each tool's own rules as in its `color_picker_apply`, never limited to a slider's soft range; its one bound is the parameter's hard limits, darktable's `$MIN` and `$MAX`, which the session already holds every value to ([0021](0021-the-operation-interface.md), [0030](0030-controls-as-darktables.md)), a hue wrapped onto its turn.
- **The interface,** the wireframe's Pickers and On-photo overlays frames: two doors to one picker, `PickerButton` in the design system, pressed while on, right-click or Ctrl-click (Cmd-click on the Mac) for an area, and the picker's cursor in the Toolbar's well, chosen exactly while the picker is on ([0104](0104-cursors-one-chosen-at-a-time-in-a-well-on-the-toolbar.md)); the marks on the photo in one overlay language for every mark, darktable's line and handles in points, darktable's logical pixels, with normal, selected and secondary weights, colour only for a sampled colour and a mask's display colour ([gui.md](../gui.md#on-photo-overlays)), the active area or point labelled with its tool and, where the tool has several, its picker; darktable's pointer gestures under [0105](0105-picker-gestures-a-click-within-6-points-the-pickers-own-kinds.md)'s rules (planned): a press a click while within 6 points, the picker's declared kinds deciding what a click and a drag make, a click's area a fixed size on screen and no switch on the photo; a right-click resetting an area as darktable's, named in the viewer bar, with Delete its key, the photo's menu not opening then; over the photo the crosshair with the picker's cursor icon beside it; Esc ending the picker wherever the focus is and A held panning over it, as darktable's force-pan; keys of Apertrust's own; turning on and off said politely to screen readers, with the reason where the person did not ask; the Colour picker as a utility tool of the Tools panel, hidden by default, its place to be decided.

## Options considered

- **The client reading the sample and sending the writes** as a `Step`: every client would need the tools' code and would race the session's edits. The session already runs pure tool code.
- **Pickers as operations' readouts** ([0019](0019-scopes-and-readouts-counted-on-the-gpu.md)): the same reduction copied into every operation crate, planned in every plan, and keyed with no region; one engine kernel serves them all, the frame and the colour checker too.
- **Reading the image back to the CPU,** as darktable's OpenCL path copies the box: a host round trip against the GPU-resident pipeline ([0008](0008-a-gpu-resident-fp32-pipeline-on-wgpu.md)).
- **Measuring the zoomed view's region:** an area outside the visible part would measure nothing, and a pick would change with the zoom.
- **A downscaled mosaic for White balance,** as darktable's preview: a pipe Apertrust does not have, against [0063](0063-departures-from-darktable-and-views-below-full-scale.md).
- **Regions in the frame's pixels, or in the original image's coordinates on the wire:** the first changes with the zoom and the window; the second needs every client to map through the geometry.
- **Floating-point atomics:** not portable in WGSL, and their order would change the mean from run to run.
- **Applying every reading, darktable's feedback aside:** an output picker would chase its own write.
- **A one-shot pick as a sequence of the other commands,** `On`, an `Area` and `Off`: `On` applies a reading at the picker's last place first, a step of its own; `Off` drops the reading still on its way, so the client must wait for it between them; the picker's kept place moves, and its marks, focus and viewer bar words flash on and off. Two or three steps where one is wanted.
- **Flags on `On`,** a region and "once": one command with two lives, every rule of `On`'s to be read twice; a variant of its own states its own.
- **A one-shot click read at the point alone,** one pixel, for an area picker: on the mosaic a pixel holds one CFA colour, so White balance would read nothing for the other two, and Exposure would read one pixel's noise; darktable's own press makes an area there.
- **A one-shot pick taking a point alone,** as a menu item at the pointer would send: a client could not pick an area it names.
- **The client making a click's area:** every client would need darktable's rule to pick where the app does; the session, which knows the photo's size, makes it from the point, with the function the app's press uses.
- **Merging quick one-shots by their picker,** as a drag's picks merge: each is one deliberate press, with an Undo of its own.
- **Each reading its own undo record,** as darktable's: a drag would make dozens of steps, against [0078](0078-undo-redo-and-the-edits-history.md).

## Consequences

- `apertrust-tools` gains `Picker`, the button's places, now a `Picker` end item ([0107](0107-packed-lines-one-line-row-with-end-items.md)), and `ProcessingTool::pick`; the engine a `picker` module, the kernel, the commands and two facts, the staging pair shared as `readback.rs`; `apertrust-ui` the overlay primitives and `PickerButton`; the app the marks, the pointer and keys, and the Colour picker tool; the settings schema the pickers' settings and the Colour picker's.
- [0016](0016-geometry-for-operations-that-move-pixels.md) maps a picked area by darktable's sorted, averaged corners; [0019](0019-scopes-and-readouts-counted-on-the-gpu.md) leaves areas to this decision and gains the scopes' restriction to the Colour picker's selection; [0032](0032-tools-off-until-first-touched.md) turns a switched tool on as its picker turns on; [0078](0078-undo-redo-and-the-edits-history.md) makes a picker a step's target, its writes the session's own.
- The one-shot pick (79.17) adds `PickerCommand::Once` to the session and `Region::pressed` to `apertrust-tools`, for scripts, the MCP and assistants; a picker cursor sends `On` and `Off` ([0104](0104-cursors-one-chosen-at-a-time-in-a-well-on-the-toolbar.md)), and no menu item picks at the pointer ([0091](0091-context-menus-places-and-tools-items-as-actions.md)).
- Each tool's page names its pickers; the roadmap's Phase 2 lists the pull requests 79.1 to 79.17, the overlay primitives before any mark on the photo; the masks' overlays in Phase 4 use the same primitives.
