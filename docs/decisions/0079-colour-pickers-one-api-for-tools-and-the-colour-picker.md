# 0079. Colour pickers, one API for tools and the Colour picker

## Context

darktable's modules pick values from the photo: Exposure, White balance, Colour calibration, Colour balance RGB, AgX, RGB curve and RGB levels in the first pass, Filmic RGB, the colour equalizer and blending later. Its color picker utility shows the colour under a point or an area and keeps samples to compare. Apertrust's definitions already hold the pickers' quads, drawn nowhere ([0029](0029-a-tool-is-darktables-page.md)), and [0019](0019-scopes-and-readouts-counted-on-the-gpu.md) left a readout over an area open. How are pickers turned on and moved, where and how is the photo measured, what does a module do with a reading, and how do the button, the marks on the photo and the utility look?

darktable 5.6.2, the reference ([0002](0002-rewrite-darktable-first-then-apertrusts-own-way.md)), keeps one active picker, a module's or the utility's (`src/gui/color_picker_proxy.c`); measures the focused module's input and, with `DT_COLOR_PICKER_IO`, its output in the preview pipe, per channel mean, minimum and maximum in the module's colour space, the mosaic per CFA colour, optionally denoised (`src/common/color_picker.c`, `src/develop/pixelpipe_hb.c:1182-1198`, `:1568-1588`); applies a reading only when the area moved or the picker started (`color_picker_proxy.c:76-104`, `:288-328`); and draws and drags the point and area in the darkroom (`src/views/darkroom.c:331-470`, `:3815-4140`).

## Decision

**One active picker per session, a module's or the Colour picker module's, measured on the GPU at its module's place in the live viewport pipe, its reading applied by the session through the module's own pure function as one change of source Auto.** The design in full, with its types, darktable's behaviour cited line by line, the component, the module, the tests and the plan, is [pickers.md](../pickers.md). Built: 79.1, the Colour picker module's own picker through the session, `Command::Picker` with `On`, `Point`, `Area`, `Switch`, `Reset` and `Off` and `Event::Picker` with its frame part, its region kept through the geometry, measured on the frame by the engine's reduction and read back through the staging pair the scopes share; and 79.2, the modules crate's side, `Definition::pickers` with their checks, `Quad::Picker(id)`, `Row::Pickers` and `Module::pick`, Exposure's, Colour balance RGB's and AgX's sliders naming their pickers, none picking yet; and 79.3, the engine's side of module pickers, the reduction at an operation's input and output in the render's encoder or a sample-only render, the mosaic per CFA colour and denoise, a reading applied through the module's `pick`, a drag's one change, within the moved area's budgets on the M4. The rest is planned, in Phase 2.

- **The protocol:** `Command::Picker(PickerCommand)` (`On { picker, area, keys, tools }`, `Point`, `Area` with `done`, `Switch`, `Reset`, `Tools`, `Off`) and `Command::Samples(SamplesCommand)` (`Add`, `Lock`, `Load`, `Store`, `Remove`, `Clear`), view state, not commands on the edit; regions in the photo's coordinates, 0 to 1 across the photo as export renders it, kept by the session in the original image's coordinates through each geometry's maps ([0016](0016-geometry-for-operations-that-move-pixels.md)), as darktable keeps them. Two facts: `Event::Picker { through, reading }`, the active picker's region as drawn, its module's input and output `Stats` (mean, min and max of four channels) and what its pick shows, and the frame's RGB and Lab under it, or a `Picker` with no reading as it turns off, its `through` every command applied then; `Event::Samples`, every stored sample's.
- **Only one picker on at a time,** as darktable's `picker_proxy`. Stored samples, darktable's "live samples", coexist with it, each measured on every frame unless locked. Turning a module's picker on turns its module on, as darktable's does; the session turns it off on `Off` or another `On`, another photo, any change to its module but its own picks, unless it is darktable's "keep-active", and its module turning off; the client turns it off as focus moves.
- **Measured on the GPU:** the engine's own reduction kernel, `crates/engine/slang/picker.slang`, no operation's pass, pairwise sums in a fixed order, darktable's B-spline denoise, its spaces (the operation's own, LCh, HSL, JzCzhz), several boxes a dispatch. A module picker's runs right after its operation's last pass in the render's encoder while its key changes; a moved area with no edit runs a sample-only render from the newest cache point at or before the operation, the picker's operation being the edited instance, so after the downscale an input reading reads the edit cache directly. The frame's samples run in a submission of their own after the frame's copy and the scopes' count, on the fitted frame or, in a zoomed view, the display output of the small whole-image pipe the scopes read, still open in [0019](0019-scopes-and-readouts-counted-on-the-gpu.md), which pickers in a zoomed view depend on. Read back through the scopes' and readouts' staging pair, never waited for; a failure turns pickers off, never the frame.
- **The scale is the fitted viewport's:** the viewport's size after the downscale, full resolution in the raw stage, never a zoomed view's region; darktable's preview pipe's place under [0063](0063-departures-from-darktable-and-views-below-full-scale.md). Exports, stills, tiles, golden cases and derivatives never measure.
- **The module side:** `Definition::pickers`, each a `Picker { id, operation, kind, denoise, output, space, keep, writes, tools }` from darktable's flags; `Quad::Picker(id)` on a slider or choice row and `Row::Pickers` for darktable's picker buttons; `Module::pick`, pure, from a reading, the values, the image context, the tool settings, the channel the module shows, the module's last reading and the keys held to the controls it writes and what its panel shows. The session applies the first reading after `On` and each reading after a move, darktable's rule, never one an upstream edit caused; a drag's readings are one gesture, its target the picker ([0078](0078-undo-redo-and-the-edits-history.md)). A picked value is stored as darktable stores it, each module's own rules as in its `color_picker_apply`, never limited to a slider's soft range; its one bound is the parameter's hard limits, darktable's `$MIN` and `$MAX`, which the session already holds every value to ([0021](0021-the-operation-interface.md), [0030](0030-controls-as-darktables.md)), a hue wrapped onto its turn.
- **The interface,** the wireframe's Pickers and On-photo overlays frames: `PickerButton` in the design system, pressed while on, right-click or Ctrl-click (Cmd-click on the Mac) for an area; the marks on the photo in one overlay language for every mark, darktable's line and handles in points, darktable's logical pixels, with normal, selected and secondary weights, colour only for a sampled colour and a mask's display colour ([gui.md](../gui.md#on-photo-overlays)); darktable's pointer, and keys of Apertrust's own; the Colour picker module as a section of the side panel, hidden by default, its place to be decided.

## Options considered

- **The client reading the sample and sending the writes** as a `Step`: every client would need the modules' code and would race the session's edits. The session already runs pure module code.
- **Pickers as operations' readouts** ([0019](0019-scopes-and-readouts-counted-on-the-gpu.md)): the same reduction copied into every operation crate, planned in every plan, and keyed with no region; one engine kernel serves them all, the frame and the colour checker too.
- **Reading the image back to the CPU,** as darktable's OpenCL path copies the box: a host round trip against the GPU-resident pipeline ([0008](0008-a-gpu-resident-fp32-pipeline-on-wgpu.md)).
- **Measuring the zoomed view's region:** an area outside the visible part would measure nothing, and a pick would change with the zoom.
- **A downscaled mosaic for White balance,** as darktable's preview: a pipe Apertrust does not have, against [0063](0063-departures-from-darktable-and-views-below-full-scale.md).
- **Regions in the frame's pixels, or in the original image's coordinates on the wire:** the first changes with the zoom and the window; the second needs every client to map through the geometry.
- **Floating-point atomics:** not portable in WGSL, and their order would change the mean from run to run.
- **Applying every reading, darktable's feedback aside:** an output picker would chase its own write.
- **Each reading its own undo record,** as darktable's: a drag would make dozens of steps, against [0078](0078-undo-redo-and-the-edits-history.md).

## Consequences

- `apertrust-modules` gains `Picker`, `Quad::Picker(id)`, `Row::Pickers` and `Module::pick`; the engine a `picker` module, the kernel, the commands and two facts, the staging pair shared as `readback.rs`; `apertrust-ui` the overlay primitives and `PickerButton`; the app the marks, the pointer and keys, and the Colour picker section; the settings schema the pickers' tool settings and the Colour picker's.
- [0016](0016-geometry-for-operations-that-move-pixels.md) maps a picked area by darktable's sorted, averaged corners; [0019](0019-scopes-and-readouts-counted-on-the-gpu.md) leaves areas to this decision and gains the scopes' restriction to the Colour picker's selection; [0032](0032-tools-off-until-first-touched.md) turns a switched module on as its picker turns on; [0078](0078-undo-redo-and-the-edits-history.md) makes a picker a step's target, its writes the session's own.
- Each module's page names its pickers; the roadmap's Phase 2 lists the pull requests 79.1 to 79.16, the overlay primitives before any mark on the photo; the masks' overlays in Phase 4 use the same primitives.
