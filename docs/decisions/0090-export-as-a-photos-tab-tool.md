# 0090. Export as a Photos-tab tool

## Context

Export holds darktable's export module row for row ([0056](0056-export-as-an-engine-job.md)). It is built as a dialog of the host ([0041](0041-dialogs-as-native-modal-windows.md)), a sheet of AppKit's on the Mac and a modal window on Windows and Linux, acting on the active photo: its rows, drawn with the side panel's controls, are about 1,000 lines and 38 tests in `crates/app/src/export.rs` and `export/tests.rs`, its dialog frame about 300 lines and 10 tests.

darktable draws its export module in the lighttable's side panel, acting on the selected images, parted by the section labels storage options, format options and global options (`src/libs/export.c` at the reference release). Its copy & import is a dialog listing the source's files (`src/libs/import.c`), its import module only buttons. In Apertrust the Photos tab already holds what is about photos as items, and the grid panel's selection already says which photos ([0037](0037-one-view-the-photos-tab.md), [0073](0073-the-photos-tabs-tools-browse-first-filters-in-folder-mode.md)); a sheet's checks would be a second way of choosing them.

The questions are where Export lives, what it acts on, what its keys do, what becomes of Import's sheet and the sheet frame, and which of the side panel's rules it bends.

## Decision

**Export is one tool of the side panel, a utility, last in the Photos tab:** darktable's export module, row for row, acting on the selection. Planned, Phase 3; the built dialog keeps working until the tool replaces it, and goes then.

### The tool

- **One utility,** id `export`, the last of the Photos tab's default tools, after Browse, Filters, Keywords, Metadata and Styles ([0073](0073-the-photos-tabs-tools-browse-first-filters-in-folder-mode.md)). It edits nothing: its values are the `export.*` settings ([0043](0043-settings-and-apertrusts-folders.md), [0056](0056-export-as-an-engine-job.md)), never the edit, its history or the sidecar ([0028](0028-tools-bound-to-operations.md) is unchanged). No on switch, no kept markers, no history step.
- **darktable's sections are groups inside it,** under darktable's labels in sentence case, in darktable's order:
  - **Storage options** and **Format options** (`export-storage-options`, `export-format-options`) are plain titled groups, always open, as darktable's section labels are: where and what Export writes is always in view.
  - **Global options** (`export-global-options`) is a collapsible group, **open at first**, an exception to [0029](0029-a-tool-is-darktables-page.md)'s "closed at first": the size it sets is the next thing the export depends on, and closing it is the person's choice.
  - **Metadata**, darktable's "edit metadata exportation" dialog brought inline, and **Multi-preset export** are collapsible groups, closed at first, drawn disabled until they are built.
  - The collapsible groups' open state is kept in `[side_panel.groups.export]`. A collapsible group, while closed, shows the dot when a setting in it is off its default, its tooltip and description the count ("Global options: 2 set"); the plain groups need none, being always open.
- **Every row is a setting row** (`Row::Setting`'s look): choices Blended ([0042](0042-numbers-shown-typed-and-stored.md)); Allow upscaling, High quality resampling and Store masks toggles, where darktable has comboboxes of No and Yes; sliders with their marker's place left empty; a double-click or Delete resetting one row; a row or option not built drawn disabled, saying why ([0030](0030-controls-as-darktables.md)). The rows the tool panel lacks, the path, note, field and size rows and the indented toggle, are added to the design system, shared with Import's sheet.
- **The Path row is darktable's:** no visible label, the path field on one line, the content's width with the folder button (Choose…) at its end, centred on that line; a path longer than the field scrolls inside it and never wraps; to screen readers the field is named "Path", then the button. The resolved file name, "IMG_2041.ARW → …/IMG_2041.jpg" from `PreviewExport`'s names, is the field's tooltip and description, not a note under it, since the summary already says where the files go; a pattern with variables can still be checked. Where the focus photo is not the open one and the pattern uses what only a decode tells (the camera's name, the ISO, the exposure bias, the image's sizes), those variables stay as written in the tooltip, "IMG_2051.ARW → …/IMG_2051_$(EXIF.ISO).jpg", with "filled in as it is exported" after it: never a guessed name shown as the real one.
- **At the side panel's 320 points** (rows 296 wide) up to 600: the path field stays one line at every width, Return setting the value; the print sizes' label goes above the fields below about 360 points, and the pixel size's below about 300; notes wrap. These widths are the design's estimates in each language ([gui.md](../gui.md#the-tools-layout)); a spike measures them before the rows are built.
- **The header has ↺, presets and ⋯,** where another utility has ⋯ alone, History ↺ and ⋯. ↺ applies Default settings: every export setting at darktable's default, those of a storage or format not shown included, but `export.ask_before_overwrite`, the overwrite alert's, which no preset sets, and any setting `--conf` holds for the run, whose reset the store refuses ([0043](0043-settings-and-apertrusts-folders.md)).
- **Presets of the whole export,** in the header's presets menu alone, with no preset row: Default settings, then **For sharing** (JPEG, 2048 × 2048 px, sRGB) and **Full size JPEG** (JPEG, no size limit, sRGB), the rest of each at darktable's defaults, where darktable ships none, then the person's own, ✓ on the one matching, then Save as Preset…, Update, Rename… and Delete…. Pointing at a preset, or ↑ and ↓, shows its values in the rows with a banner under the header until the pointer leaves the list. Kept in `presets/export.toml`, read by the client. Until presets are built the header's presets button is disabled, saying presets come later; ↺ applies Default settings, and the summary starts with Default settings or Custom.
- **The tool ends** with a summary, a collapsible section of its own, closed by default and kept per tool as a group's state is. Closed, it is a disclosure still showing the one-line summary and the destination line, never hiding them, since it is the guard against stale settings; open, it adds a labelled list: Preset, Photos (with any view-only left out), Format (with quality or bit depth), Size (with the output size of the photo the notes describe, the active one or the first exported), Destination, On conflict (what it does, with the count). To screen readers it is a disclosure button named by the one-line summary, its expanded state reported, the list a description list. The one-line summary starts with the preset in use, or Custom when the settings match none ("For sharing · 12 photos · JPEG · 2048 px → ~/…/apertrust_exported", "Custom · 12 photos · …"), which the button's description starts with too. Then, outside the summary and always shown, the warnings in `warning` ("Replaces 3 files already there", "1 view-only photo left out") and the **Export 12 photos** button, primary, the content's full width, its words centred; and a line for its job: started, running with Show jobs, queued after another, the same photos and settings already exporting, or failed with Show jobs. **This end scrolls with the tool,** never pinned.

### What it acts on

- **The window's selection, else the active photo;** the active photo alone until Phase 3 can select several. View-only photos, whose edits a newer Apertrust wrote or whose history cannot be read, are left out and counted in a warning; with only those, the button is disabled, saying why.
- **An export leaves the selection and the tab as they were,** the focus on the button; the jobs window opens without taking the focus ([0057](0057-jobs-as-darktables-control-jobs.md)), and a notice with Show in Finder says when each export ends (with the library's notices). Export Again exports the window's selection with the settings as they stand.
- **The settings are taken when Export is pressed,** each photo's edit as it is then ([0056](0056-export-as-an-engine-job.md)); a change during a job reaches only the next.
- **No per-photo destination marks** on the grid panel: On conflict's note and the warning give the counts. Marks are revisited in the usability pass after Phase 3.
- **The overwrite alert** is as built, "Export in overwrite mode?" with Don't ask again, Cancel the default, the main window's.

### Keys and gestures

- **Export… and Ctrl+E (Cmd+E) reveal the tool:** they choose the tab holding Export, or raise its floating panel ([0083](0083-the-side-panels-layout-dragged-and-floating.md)), open it, scroll its end into view and focus its button with the focus ring. Return then exports; Esc gives the focus back to where Ctrl+E found it. Ctrl+Return (Cmd+Return) anywhere in the tool presses Export while it is enabled; Return on a row keeps the row's own meaning. The button's access key is &Export.
- **File › Export Again exports at once** with the current settings, darktable's Ctrl+E, its default key **Ctrl+Shift+E (Cmd+Shift+E)**.
- **The scroll wheel:** a plain scroll always scrolls the side panel; Option+scroll on the Mac and Ctrl+scroll on Windows and Linux change only the focused export row, never the row under the pointer.
- **Export stays in every workspace:** Customise tabs, a drag and Move to Tab may move it, but none removes it from a workspace's last tab holding it, and a settings file that hides it or leaves it out gets it back as [0083](0083-the-side-panels-layout-dragged-and-floating.md) places a section no tab holds, so Ctrl+E always has a place to reveal it.
- **Edit › Previous Selection** goes back one level, to the selection as it was before its last change, with its active photo: the way back from a stray click that drops a large selection. Its default key is **Ctrl+Shift+D (Cmd+Shift+D)**, beyond darktable, where that key is "duplicate virgin": New Virtual Copy from Original gets another key when virtual copies are designed. It comes with Phase 3's selection.

### The session

Nothing new: `Command::Export { photos, settings, places }` and `PreviewExport { photos, settings, places, focus }` ([0056](0056-export-as-an-engine-job.md)), its `focus` the window's active photo, or the first photo exported where the active one is left out (planned), so the notes always describe a photo that goes. The preview is sent on a change of a setting, the selection or the active photo, as the tool comes into view, when the window regains the focus and when an export job ends, since its files change what is already there, never while the tool is closed or out of view. The tool keeps the number of its latest request and shows an answer only when its `through` is that number or later; an answer to an earlier request, arriving after a change, is dropped, so its counts never fill notes meant for newer settings. `through` counts the session's commands, so in a session several clients share (decision 71, planned) another client's later `PreviewExport` would pass that check: there the `ExportPreview` fact names the client that asked and is a topic of that client's own, so one client's answer never supersedes another's before it arrives, and each tool takes only its own client's answers; the session keeps and cancels each client's pending preview apart, so a new request cancels only its own client's earlier one. Its answers become the path field's tooltip, the `focus` photo's file name in full; notes under their rows, what On conflict does with the files already there and the output size; and the counts in the warning.

### Import

- **Import stays a full-window sheet** ([0085](0085-import-in-folder-mode.md), [0086](0086-import-as-a-full-window-sheet.md)), with no Import tool. Browse's top line gains **Import…** after ＋, which opens the sheet; where the side panel is too narrow for it, it becomes ＋'s first item.
- **Open:** a small Import tool holding import's defaults (the metadata, rating and labels stamped at import, its presets) may earn its place later.
- **The sheet frame serves Import alone,** and a tool's place is the side panel or Import ([0004](0004-tools-for-people-operations-for-algorithms.md)).

## Options considered

- **Keep the Export sheet** ([0084](0084-export-as-a-sheet-of-export-tools.md)): checks beside the window's selection are two ways of choosing the same photos; the whole window for a task whose photos are chosen already; a frame built for two sheets that only Import would use.
- **Five export tools in the side panel,** the sheet's column moved: five headers and five ↺ for one task, which a layout could scatter across tabs; darktable has one module.
- **Import and Export both as tools, the card's photos in the grid panel:** the grid panel shows what is open, and a card's listing there would mix a source with it, checks with the selection; darktable's copy & import is a dialog.
- **The photos exported written back as the selection,** as the sheet would have: the tool exports the selection, so there is nothing to write back, and Export Again repeats it as it stands.
- **Ctrl+E exporting at once,** darktable's: a key that writes files with the settings out of sight. Export Again keeps darktable's behaviour on its own key.
- **The summary's list always open:** about 100 points more at every look for detail the one line already gives; **no summary:** the preset in use and where the files go would be read only from rows that may be scrolled away, and the button's description would have nothing to repeat.
- **The end pinned at the side panel's foot:** about 120 points of the panel taken in every tab, and a second scrolling region; Ctrl+E brings the end into view anyway.
- **An Import tool now,** holding the sheet's defaults: settings the sheet already shows; left open.
- **Every group collapsible,** closed at first as [0029](0029-a-tool-is-darktables-page.md) has it, or open at first: closed, the first look shows headers only and where the files go is out of sight; open but collapsible, a closed Storage or Format options hides the settings an export can least afford to get wrong.
- **No group collapsible,** darktable's section labels for all five: Metadata and Multi-preset export, long and rarely changed, would always take the panel's height.
- **A Preset row at the tool's top** as well as the header's menu: a second control for one choice, and a row's height; the summary over the button already names the preset in use where the export is pressed.
- **The header's ⋯ alone,** as other utilities': presets and a reset are what export settings are changed by most.
- **Keep the dialog:** a modal window over the photo for a task darktable keeps in its panel, and a second form beside the side panel's.

## Consequences

- `crates/app/src/export.rs` becomes the Export tool's section; its rows and their tests carry over. The dialog's frame goes: AppKit's sheet and the modal window, the 44-point header, the 200-point Presets column, the footer and Close, `impl Dialog for Export` and its snapshots. The dialog host stays, for Settings, the jobs and log windows, Linux's alerts, the prompts and [0089](0089-one-instance-per-user-data-folder-a-window-per-place.md)'s windows.
- `apertrust-export`, the `UserExport` job and the `export.*` settings are unchanged. Export Again gains its key, and with Phase 3's selection it sends the selected photos where it sends the open photo alone today. `PreviewExport` gains only its `focus`, planned since [0056](0056-export-as-an-engine-job.md).
- `apertrust-ui` gains the setting rows, a `Button` with its words centred, and a utility's header with ↺ and presets. The sheets' `export.column_width`, `export.order`, `export.keep_open` and `export.thumbnails` are never added.
- [0084](0084-export-as-a-sheet-of-export-tools.md) is superseded. [0086](0086-import-as-a-full-window-sheet.md) is Import's alone; [0041](0041-dialogs-as-native-modal-windows.md) lists Import and Print as sheets and Export as a tool; [0056](0056-export-as-an-engine-job.md)'s `focus` is the active photo; [0073](0073-the-photos-tabs-tools-browse-first-filters-in-folder-mode.md) ends the Photos tab with Export; [0029](0029-a-tool-is-darktables-page.md) records Global options, open at first, as the one exception to "closed at first"; [0004](0004-tools-for-people-operations-for-algorithms.md), [0037](0037-one-view-the-photos-tab.md), [0042](0042-numbers-shown-typed-and-stored.md), [0083](0083-the-side-panels-layout-dragged-and-floating.md) and [0085](0085-import-in-folder-mode.md) follow; so do the scroll wheel's rule and the default keyboard map in [gui.md](../gui.md#the-scroll-wheel-over-a-row).
- The first milestone: the rows, Default settings, the tool's end and Ctrl+E's reveal, the dialog removed; then presets, Multi-preset export, Metadata and Style ([gui.md](../gui.md#the-build-in-cards)). A spike first measures the rows at 320 points in the four languages and the pseudo-locale.
- [gui.md](../gui.md#the-export-tool) describes the tool; the wireframe's Export board is redrawn as the tool, and the `ui-conventions` and `desktop-architecture` skills are to describe it.
