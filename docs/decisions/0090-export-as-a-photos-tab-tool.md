# 0090. Export as a Photos-tab tool

## Context

Export holds darktable's export module row for row ([0056](0056-export-as-an-engine-job.md)). It is built as a tool of the Photos tab acting on the active photo, its rows drawn with the Tools panel's controls in `crates/app/src/export.rs`, tested in `export/tests.rs`; its groups, Summary, warnings and button states are built; what is planned is the selection, the keys, the preview's in-view rule, presets and metadata.

darktable draws its export module in the lighttable's side panel, acting on the selected images, parted by the section labels storage options, format options and global options (`src/libs/export.c` at the reference release). Its copy & import is a dialog listing the source's files (`src/libs/import.c`), its import module only buttons. In Apertrust the Photos tab already holds what is about photos as items, and the Browser's selection already says which photos ([0037](0037-one-view-the-photos-tab.md), [0073](0073-the-photos-tabs-tools-browse-first-filters-in-folder-mode.md)); a sheet's checks would be a second way of choosing them.

The questions are where Export lives, what it acts on, what its keys do, what becomes of Import's sheet and the sheet frame, and which of the Tools panel's rules it bends.

## Decision

**Export is one tool of the Tools panel, a utility, last in the Photos tab:** darktable's export module, row for row, acting on the selection. Phase 3, its first parts built, for the active photo: the section with its groups, the path on one line, the header's ↺ and disabled presets button, the Summary, the warnings and the button in each of its states, which Export… and Ctrl+E reveal; the selection, the keys, the preview's rule and presets are planned.

### The tool

- **One utility,** id `export`, the last of the Photos tab's default tools, after Collections, Filters, Keywords, Metadata and Styles ([0073](0073-the-photos-tabs-tools-browse-first-filters-in-folder-mode.md)). It edits nothing: its values are the `export.*` settings ([0043](0043-settings-and-apertrusts-folders.md), [0056](0056-export-as-an-engine-job.md)), never the edit, its history or the sidecar ([0028](0028-tools-bound-to-operations.md) is unchanged). No on switch, no kept markers, no history step.
- **darktable's sections are groups inside it,** under darktable's labels in sentence case, in darktable's order:
  - **Storage options** and **Format options** (`export-storage-options`, `export-format-options`) are plain titled groups, always open, as darktable's section labels are: where and what Export writes is always in view.
  - **Global options** (`export-global-options`) is a collapsible group, **open at first**, an exception to [0029](0029-a-tool-is-darktables-page.md)'s "closed at first": the size it sets is the next thing the export depends on, and closing it is the person's choice.
  - **Metadata**, darktable's "edit metadata exportation" dialog brought inline, and **Multi-preset export** are collapsible groups, closed at first, drawn disabled until they are built.
  - The collapsible groups' open state is kept in `[edit.groups.export]`, planned to move to `[tools_panel.groups.export]` ([0095](0095-the-windows-parts-named-by-their-role.md)). A collapsible group, while closed, shows the dot when a setting in it is off its default, its tooltip and description the count ("Global options: 2 set"); the plain groups need none, being always open.
- **Every row is a setting row** (`Row::Setting`'s look): choices Blended ([0042](0042-numbers-shown-typed-and-stored.md)); Allow upscaling, High quality resampling and Store masks choices of No and Yes, as darktable has them; sliders with their marker's place left empty; a double-click or Delete resetting one row; a row or option not built drawn disabled, saying why ([0030](0030-controls-as-darktables.md)). The rows the tool panel lacks, the path, note, field and size rows and the indented toggle, are added to the design system, shared with Import's sheet.
- **Its page is a utility's declaration** ([0093](0093-one-tool-model-processing-tools-and-utility-tools.md)): a `UtilityDefinition` in `apertrust-tools`, drawn with every tool's header and page drawer (step 93.6, built), before Import's sheet shares its rows: its settings are setting rows naming the `export.*` settings by their keys with its own words, a test failing on a key not in the settings schema, the format-specific rows shown only for their formats; Target storage, the path line, the size line, the groups to come and the Summary with the tool's end are custom rows `export.rs` draws; the app binds the rows to `apertrust-export`'s settings, on which `apertrust-tools` never depends.
- **The Path row is darktable's:** no visible label, the path field on one line, the content's width with the folder button (Choose…) at its end, centred on that line; a path longer than the field scrolls inside it and never wraps; to screen readers the field is named "Path", then the button. The resolved file name, "IMG_2041.ARW → …/IMG_2041.jpg" from `PreviewExport`'s names, is the field's tooltip and description, not a note under it, since the Summary already says where the files go; a pattern with variables can still be checked. Where the focus photo is not the open one and the pattern uses what only a decode tells (the camera's name, the ISO, the exposure bias, the image's sizes), those variables stay as written in the tooltip, "IMG_2051.ARW → …/IMG_2051_$(EXIF.ISO).jpg", with "filled in as it is exported" after it: never a guessed name shown as the real one.
- **At the Tools panel's 320 points** (rows 296 wide) up to 600: the path field stays one line at every width, Return setting the value; the size row has no label at any width, Set size above it naming the unit, one line of `[w] × [h] px` or `[w] × [h] cm @ [dpi] dpi` whose fields shrink to fit, and its group named "Size in pixels", "Size in centimetres" or "Size in inches" for screen readers; problems under a row wrap. These widths are the design's estimates in each language ([gui.md](../gui.md#the-tools-layout)); a spike measures them before the rows are built.
- **The header has ↺, presets and ⋯,** where another utility has ⋯ alone, History ↺ and ⋯, each declared in its `ToolDefinition` (93). ↺ applies Default settings, the utility's own reset: every export setting at darktable's default, those of a storage or format not shown included, but `export.ask_before_overwrite`, the overwrite alert's, which no preset sets, and any setting `--conf` holds for the run, whose reset the store refuses ([0043](0043-settings-and-apertrusts-folders.md)).
- **Presets of the whole export,** presets of every `export.*` setting its page stores, its custom rows' (the path, the size line) included (93), in the header's presets menu alone, with no preset row: Default settings, then **For sharing** (JPEG, 2048 × 2048 px, sRGB) and **Full size JPEG** (JPEG, no size limit, sRGB), the rest of each at darktable's defaults, where darktable ships none, then the person's own, ✓ on the one matching, then Save as Preset…, Update, Rename… and Delete…. Pointing at a preset, or ↑ and ↓, shows its values in the rows with a banner under the header until the pointer leaves the list. Kept in `presets/export.toml`, read by the client. They come with the presets API every tool shares, processing and utility alike, as darktable's are, a decision of its own (planned). Until then the header's presets button is disabled, saying presets come later; ↺ applies Default settings, and the Summary's Preset row reads Default settings or Custom.
- **The tool ends** with a **Summary**, a collapsible group like Global options, open at first and kept per tool as a group's state is, drawn only while a photo can be exported: a labelled list of what an export would write, one line a row, the labels right-aligned in a column and the values after it, cut at their end (a destination in its middle) and whole in their tooltips. Its rows: Preset (the preset in use, or Custom when the settings match none), File name (the name the session resolved), Size (the output size, with the physical size in the print modes), Scale (only by scale), Profile, Format (with its quality or bit depth) and Destination. To screen readers each row is a label reading as "Size 6000 × 4000 px". Below it, outside the group and always shown, the warnings in `warning` ("Replaces 3 files already there", "1 view-only photo left out") and the **Export 12 photos** button, primary, the content's full width, its words centred, and for a failed export its line in `error` with Show jobs; no line says that an export started or runs, the jobs window showing it. **This end scrolls with the tool,** never pinned.

### What it acts on

- **The window's selection, else the active photo;** the active photo alone until Phase 3 can select several. View-only photos, whose edits a newer Apertrust wrote or whose history cannot be read, are left out and counted in a warning; with only those, the button is disabled, saying why.
- **An export leaves the selection and the tab as they were,** the focus on the button; the jobs window opens without taking the focus ([0057](0057-jobs-as-darktables-control-jobs.md)), and a notice with Show in Finder says when each export ends (with the library's notices). Export Again exports the window's selection with the settings as they stand.
- **The settings are taken when Export is pressed,** each photo's edit as it is then ([0056](0056-export-as-an-engine-job.md)); a change during a job reaches only the next.
- **No per-photo destination marks** on the Browser: On conflict's note and the warning give the counts. Marks are revisited in the usability pass after Phase 3.
- **The overwrite alert** is as built, "Export in overwrite mode?" with Don't ask again, Cancel the default, the main window's.

### Keys and gestures

- **Export… and Ctrl+E (Cmd+E) reveal the tool:** they choose the tab holding Export, or raise its floating panel ([0083](0083-the-side-panels-layout-dragged-and-floating.md)), open it, scroll its end into view and focus its button with the focus ring. Return then exports; Esc gives the focus back to where Ctrl+E found it. Ctrl+Return (Cmd+Return) anywhere in the tool presses Export while it is enabled; Return on a row keeps the row's own meaning. The button's access key is &Export.
- **File › Export Again exports at once** with the current settings, darktable's Ctrl+E, its default key **Ctrl+Shift+E (Cmd+Shift+E)**.
- **The scroll wheel:** a plain scroll always scrolls the Tools panel; Option+scroll on the Mac and Ctrl+scroll on Windows and Linux change only the focused export row, never the row under the pointer.
- **Export stays in every workspace, never hidden:** the visual editor, a drag and Move to Tab may move it to another tab, where it then is, but none leaves it in no tab: ⋯ › Hide is disabled for it, the editor removes no tab while it is Export's only one, and a workspace's file that hides it or leaves it out gets it back at the end of the Photos tab where it is shown, else of the first tab that holds tools ([0096](0096-workspaces-as-files-hidden-tools-and-finding-a-tool.md)), so Ctrl+E always has a place to reveal it.
- **Edit › Previous Selection** goes back one level, to the selection as it was before its last change, with its active photo: the way back from a stray click that drops a large selection. Its default key is **Ctrl+Shift+D (Cmd+Shift+D)**, beyond darktable, where that key is "duplicate virgin": New Virtual Copy from Original gets another key when virtual copies are designed. It comes with Phase 3's selection.

### The session

Nothing new: `Command::Export { photos, settings, places }` and `PreviewExport { photos, settings, places, focus }` ([0056](0056-export-as-an-engine-job.md)), its `focus` the window's active photo, or the first photo exported where the active one is left out (planned), so the notes always describe a photo that goes. The preview is sent on a change of a setting, the selection or the active photo, as the tool comes into view, when the window regains the focus and when an export job ends, since its files change what is already there, never while the tool is closed or out of view. The tool keeps the number of its latest request and shows an answer only when its `through` is that number or later; an answer to an earlier request, arriving after a change, is dropped, so its counts never fill notes meant for newer settings. `through` counts the session's commands, so in a session several clients share (decision 71, planned) another client's later `PreviewExport` would pass that check: there the `ExportPreview` fact names the client that asked and is a topic of that client's own, so one client's answer never supersedes another's before it arrives, and each tool takes only its own client's answers; the session keeps and cancels each client's pending preview apart, so a new request cancels only its own client's earlier one. Its answers become the path field's tooltip, the `focus` photo's file name in full; notes under their rows, what On conflict does with the files already there and the output size; and the counts in the warning.

### Import

- **Import stays a full-window sheet** ([0085](0085-import-in-folder-mode.md), [0086](0086-import-as-a-full-window-sheet.md)), with no Import tool. Collections' top line gains **Import…** after ＋, which opens the sheet; where the Tools panel is too narrow for it, it becomes ＋'s first item.
- **Open:** a small Import tool holding import's defaults (the metadata, rating and labels stamped at import, its presets) may earn its place later.
- **The sheet frame serves Import alone,** and a tool's place is the Tools panel or Import ([0004](0004-tools-for-people-operations-for-algorithms.md)).

## Options considered

- **Keep the Export sheet** ([0084](0084-export-as-a-sheet-of-export-tools.md)): checks beside the window's selection are two ways of choosing the same photos; the whole window for a task whose photos are chosen already; a frame built for two sheets that only Import would use.
- **Five export tools in the Tools panel,** the sheet's column moved: five headers and five ↺ for one task, which a layout could scatter across tabs; darktable has one module.
- **Import and Export both as tools, the card's photos in the Browser:** the Browser shows what is open, and a card's listing there would mix a source with it, checks with the selection; darktable's copy & import is a dialog.
- **The photos exported written back as the selection,** as the sheet would have: the tool exports the selection, so there is nothing to write back, and Export Again repeats it as it stands.
- **Ctrl+E exporting at once,** darktable's: a key that writes files with the settings out of sight. Export Again keeps darktable's behaviour on its own key.
- **A one-line summary over the button, with a list behind a disclosure:** a line cut by its parts is hard to read in a narrow panel and still hides what an export writes; **no summary:** the preset in use, the file name, the size and where the files go would be read only from rows that may be scrolled away.
- **The end pinned at the Tools panel's foot:** about 120 points of the panel taken in every tab, and a second scrolling region; Ctrl+E brings the end into view anyway.
- **An Import tool now,** holding the sheet's defaults: settings the sheet already shows; left open.
- **Every group collapsible,** closed at first as [0029](0029-a-tool-is-darktables-page.md) has it, or open at first: closed, the first look shows headers only and where the files go is out of sight; open but collapsible, a closed Storage or Format options hides the settings an export can least afford to get wrong.
- **No group collapsible,** darktable's section labels for all five: Metadata and Multi-preset export, long and rarely changed, would always take the panel's height.
- **A Preset row at the tool's top** as well as the header's menu: a second control for one choice, and a row's height; the Summary over the button already names the preset in use where the export is pressed.
- **The header's ⋯ alone,** as other utilities': presets and a reset are what export settings are changed by most.
- **Keep the dialog:** a modal window over the photo for a task darktable keeps in its panel, and a second form beside the Tools panel's.

## Consequences

- `crates/app/src/export.rs` is the Export tool's section. The dialog host serves Settings, the jobs and log windows, Linux's alerts, the prompts and [0089](0089-one-instance-per-user-data-folder-a-window-per-place.md)'s windows, and no Export.
- `apertrust-export`, the `UserExport` job and the `export.*` settings are unchanged. Export Again gains its key, and with Phase 3's selection it sends the selected photos where it sends the open photo alone today. `PreviewExport` gains only its `focus`, planned since [0056](0056-export-as-an-engine-job.md).
- `apertrust-ui` gains the setting rows and a `Button` with its words centred; the header is every tool's, drawn from its `ToolDefinition` with the buttons it declares (93). The sheets' `export.column_width`, `export.order`, `export.keep_open` and `export.thumbnails` are never added.
- [0084](0084-export-as-a-sheet-of-export-tools.md) is superseded. [0086](0086-import-as-a-full-window-sheet.md) is Import's alone; [0041](0041-dialogs-as-native-modal-windows.md) lists Import and Print as sheets and Export as a tool; [0056](0056-export-as-an-engine-job.md)'s `focus` is the active photo; [0073](0073-the-photos-tabs-tools-browse-first-filters-in-folder-mode.md) ends the Photos tab with Export; [0029](0029-a-tool-is-darktables-page.md) records Global options, open at first, as the one exception to "closed at first"; [0004](0004-tools-for-people-operations-for-algorithms.md), [0037](0037-one-view-the-photos-tab.md), [0042](0042-numbers-shown-typed-and-stored.md), [0083](0083-the-side-panels-layout-dragged-and-floating.md) and [0085](0085-import-in-folder-mode.md) follow; so do the scroll wheel's rule and the default keyboard map in [gui.md](../gui.md#the-scroll-wheel-over-a-row).
- Built, for the active photo: the tool with its groups, the Summary, the warnings and the button's states at the tool's end, the rows with darktable's choices of No and Yes, the one-line size row, Default settings and Ctrl+E's reveal. Planned: the new keys, the preview's in-view rule, the selection, then presets, Multi-preset export, Metadata and Style ([gui.md](../gui.md#the-build-in-steps)).
- [gui.md](../gui.md#the-export-tool) describes the tool; the wireframe's Export board is redrawn as the tool, and the `ui-conventions` and `desktop-architecture` skills are to describe it.
