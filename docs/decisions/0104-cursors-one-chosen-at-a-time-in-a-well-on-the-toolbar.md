# 0104. Cursors: one chosen at a time, in a well on the Toolbar

## Context

A press on the photo can mean many things: pan a zoomed photo, pick a neutral area for White balance, set Exposure from an area, paint a mask, heal a spot, mark the photo with a note, apply one photo's edit to another. darktable at the reference release has no shared way to choose between them: its pickers are turned on from each module's picker button, its masks drawn from the mask manager and each module's drawn-mask buttons, and a drag pans unless a module takes it. A quick pick could also be an item in the photo's right-click menu, "Set White Balance from Here", which needs a pick at one point as one completed change, an open question of [0091](0091-context-menus-places-and-tools-items-as-actions.md).

The question is how a person chooses what a press on the photo does, for pickers now and masks, retouch, annotations and copying edits later, with one API every owner uses, without changing darktable's own picker button and without the engine learning anything new. For pickers it is also how a cursor relates to the tool's own picker: darktable's picker is live while it is on and ends on many triggers, and a cursor that picked on its own terms would give one tool's picking two models, two looks and two lifetimes. It is product scope darktable does not cover ([0002](0002-rewrite-darktable-first-then-apertrusts-own-way.md)), on the Toolbar ([0092](0092-the-toolbar.md)), with keys after darktable ([0098](0098-actions-and-shortcuts-after-darktable.md)) and the picker commands of [0079](0079-colour-pickers-one-api-for-tools-and-the-colour-picker.md).

## Decision

**One cursor is chosen at a time; it decides what a press on the photo does.** Select, Pan and the later groups' cursors stay chosen until another is chosen. **A picker cursor is a second door to its tool's picker,** darktable's as [0079](0079-colour-pickers-one-api-for-tools-and-the-colour-picker.md) has it: choosing it sends the same `PickerCommand::On` as pressing the tool's picker button, and it is chosen exactly while that picker is on. The cursors sit in one sunken well on the Toolbar, one button per group. Cursors are an additional speed-up, never the single way to a feature: tools keep their own buttons. The design in full, with its measures, words, keys, API and plan, is [cursors.md](../cursors.md). Built in Phase 2: the one-shot pick (104.1), the tools' declarations with White balance's and Exposure's picker cursors (104.2), the well in the design system (104.3) and the app's Select, Pan and Pick (White balance, Exposure) on the Toolbar (104.4), whose picker cursors still pick once with `PickerCommand::Once`; planned in Phase 2, the well showing the picker that is on (104.5), the picker cursors as doors to `On` (104.6) and the label on the area with the pointer's badge (104.7); the rest comes with its feature.

### The groups

In the well's order, each a group of its own even with one cursor:

| Group | Cursors | A press | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Select | Select, the default at every start and Esc's target | A click selects a mark on the photo; a drag that starts on a mark moves it, one that starts on empty photo does nothing: Select never pans by a drag, Pan does, or A held with any cursor, and the scroll and the trackpad's swipe pan, and Ctrl+scroll and the pinch zoom, with every cursor | Phase 2; marks to select come with stored samples and masks |
| Pan | Pan | A drag always moves a zoomed photo, even over a mark | Phase 2 |
| Pick | White balance, Exposure, Colour calibration (built with its pick, 79.9); each other tool's picker's own as its pick is built in the app | Choosing one turns its tool's picker on, which applies at once from the area the picker holds; a click or drag is then that picker's, live, as darktable's | Phase 2 |
| Colour picker | Colour picker | Choosing it turns the Colour picker's own picker on, as a point or an area as its Start as says, at that kind's place the picker holds; a click or drag is then that picker's, live, moving the live sample, which writes nothing | Phase 2, with the Colour picker (79.16) |
| Mask | Brush, Linear gradient, Radial gradient, AI object, Path | The chosen layer's mask, one step a stroke or shape ([0034](0034-layers-masked-tool-instances-in-a-fixed-order.md)) | Phase 4 |
| Retouch | Heal, Clone | Spots on a retouch layer | Phase 4 |
| Annotate | Pen, Arrow, Text | A note on the photo | Later, its own decision |
| Copy and apply | Copy and apply | A click on a photo, in the Viewer or on a thumbnail, applies the copied edit; Alt-click copies | Phase 3, with Copy Parts |

- **Scroll and the trackpad pan** a zoomed photo with every cursor, and hold A pans from any cursor, a picker on included, darktable's own key to force panning: Pan is borrowed while A is held, its face lit, and on release the cursor chosen before is shown again, a picker still on having the photo again. Scroll with a mask or retouch cursor is provisional until masks are built.
- **What a cursor does is in its tooltip,** "White balance picker · K — sets white balance from the area it holds; drag to move it"; the viewer bar carries no help line of the cursors' own, only a picker's words while it is on (decision 79). The pointer takes the cursor's shape over the photo.
- **A picker cursor whose picker cannot turn on** (the photo not live or open for viewing only, a tool hidden by the History tab's eye, Exposure's picker toward a reference with none set) is never chosen: its face and list item are disabled with the reason, as its picker button is. **Another cursor that cannot act** stays chosen, its accent kept: a press does nothing, the pointer over the photo is the not-allowed one and the tooltip says why. With no photo shown the whole well is disabled, the chosen face dimming with it, as any disabled control.

### The well

- **One toolbar item, `cursors`,** a part of the Toolbar's ([0092](0092-the-toolbar.md)), centred between two flexible spaces after Apply edit: `… copy-edit, apply-edit, flexible-space, cursors, flexible-space, jobs, assistant, save-state`. It never folds; the actions fold before it. Where a workspace's list leaves `cursors` out, the well is not drawn, and every cursor with a key keeps it.
- **A `sunken` track** 30 points high, `radius_large`, 2 by 4 points of padding, its buttons `spacing.s` apart; client area, so a double-click on it never maximises the window.
- **One button per group, two targets, no plates** at rest, under the pointer or chosen ([0081](0081-icon-only-buttons-draw-no-plates-on-is-the-filled-icon.md)): the face, 22 by 26 points with its 16-point icon at its end, chooses the group's active cursor; the chevron, 11 by 26 right against it, opens the group's list. A group of one with no settings, Select and Pan, is one face 24 points wide with no chevron.
- **The chosen cursor's icon is in `accent_text`,** its stroke a quarter heavier than the outline (`STROKE_HEAVY`), and a cursor other than a picker's keeps that look while it cannot act now, its reason as tooltip, but dims with the whole well while no photo is shown; under the pointer an icon lifts to `text_strong`; a cursor not chosen that cannot act now is drawn disabled, `text_disabled`; the focus ring on one target. The accent is the well's alone: a tool's picker button keeps its own look while on, the filled pipette in `text_strong` ([0081](0081-icon-only-buttons-draw-no-plates-on-is-the-filled-icon.md)).
- **The list** (the popup service, [0040](0040-popup-menus-native-on-the-mac-drawn-elsewhere.md)): the group's cursors with their keys, a check on the active one, choosing one making it active and chosen; then, under a line, the group's own settings, toggles and choices its cursors share, app settings in `settings.toml`'s `[cursors.<group>]`, never history. Pick has none. Each later group's settings are chosen with that group: Copy and apply's in Phase 3, Mask's and Retouch's with Phase 4, Annotate's with its decision.
- **Amounts** (a brush's size, hardness, flow) are no list items: a second click on the chosen face opens them as slider rows, a 4-point triangle marking a face whose cursor has them; `[` and `]` change the size.

### Keys

| Cursor | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Select | Esc, where nothing focused keeps it |
| Pan | A, held |
| White balance | K |
| Exposure | None |
| Colour picker | Shift+K |
| Mask and retouch cursors | Chosen with Phase 4 |

- **A cursor's key, tapped, chooses it and it stays,** but Pan's A, which is a hold alone, as H's peek: Pan is borrowed while A is held, never kept. A picker cursor's key turns its picker on, and it stays on until the picker turns off. Held, a cursor's key borrows the cursor until released, the one before returning; a picker cursor's turns its picker on while held and off on release, but leaves on a picker already on before, the pick it made kept; held while its picker is already on, it changes nothing and leaves the picker on at release (Phase 3, with decision 98's dispatcher; Phase 2 has taps). Space stays Next Photo.
- **Esc chooses Select,** where no focused widget keeps it; while a picker is on it turns the picker off and chooses Select wherever the focus is, only a field being typed in or an open list keeping the first Esc.
- **Each cursor is an action** of the `hold` kind ([0098](0098-actions-and-shortcuts-after-darktable.md)): `on` chooses it, a picker cursor's sending its picker's `On`; `hold` borrows it; `off` chooses Select, a picker cursor's sending `Off`; `toggle` chooses it or, chosen, Select. A tool's is `tool-<tool>-cursor-<cursor>`, the others' `cursor-<id>`.

### One picker, two doors

- **A tool's picker button is darktable's, unchanged:** on, the first reading applied at once from the area the picker holds, live readings as the area moves, off. Its cursor in the well is the second door to the same picker: its face, its list item and its key send the same `On`, and a click or a drag on the photo is then the live picker's, as darktable's ([0079](0079-colour-pickers-one-api-for-tools-and-the-colour-picker.md)).
- **One state, shown together:** the picker cursor is chosen exactly while its picker is on, whichever door turned it on, the button on too. When the picker turns off for any of darktable's reasons, another picker turned on; a change to its tool other than its own picks, unless the picker is darktable's keep-active; Undo, Redo or a move through history that changes its tool; its tool turning off; another tool's control changing; another tool's Show on photo turning on (planned, with Rotate and perspective's port; one tool's on at a time); its own tool's section collapsed in the Tools panel; another photo or the photo closing; the button or the face pressed again; Esc; or Select, Pan or another cursor chosen in the well (holding A only borrows Pan: its face lit while A is held, the picker staying on and its cursor's face lit again on release), the well shows the cursor of the picker now on where another picker turned on and has one, else the cursor the person chose, else Select, in the same frame, the area goes, and screen readers hear it politely, with the reason where the person did not ask ("Exposure picker off: another photo").
- **Every tool's picker has its own cursor** in Pick once its tool's pick is built in the app, so the well always shows the picker that is on; no generic Pick face stands in for a picker without one.
- **Choosing a picker cursor neither scrolls nor opens the Tools panel:** a cursor is a quick action. The picker's area carries a small label naming its tool, and its picker where the tool has several a person can turn on ("Exposure · Reference"), a name that repeats the tool's said once ("Exposure"); over the photo the pointer is the crosshair with the cursor's icon as a badge beside it, drawn by the app's overlay, not a system custom pointer. Apertrust has no focused tool, so darktable's rule that a picker ends as its module loses focus becomes: it turns off when another tool's control changes, when another tool's Show on photo turns on (planned, with Rotate and perspective's port; one tool's on at a time), or when its own tool's section is collapsed in the Tools panel, whichever door turned it on.
- **A right-click on the photo while a picker is on is the picker's:** on a stored sample of a kind the picker takes, while samples are shown on the photo, it loads that sample (planned, 79.14); elsewhere, with an area, it resets the area, darktable's, the viewer bar naming it and Delete its key; the photo's menu ([0091](0091-context-menus-places-and-tools-items-as-actions.md)) does not open then.
- **No series across photos:** another photo ends the picker, as darktable's; its area is kept, so the next photo (Space or →, from anywhere but the picker's focused area, whose keys move it), then the cursor's key, picks the same place on it at once.
- **The Colour picker's own picker has a group of its own,** after Pick, which keeps the tools' pickers, those that change the edit: one cursor, Colour picker (`cursor-colour`, on Shift+K, its icon `pick-colour`), the second door to the picker the Colour picker section's header button turns on, with the same model and lifetime, chosen exactly while that picker is on. Its group setting is Start as, Point or Area (`cursors.colour_picker.start`), Point by default as darktable's plain click on its button: Start as always decides the kind it turns on as, the `area` of its `On`, and the picker takes that kind's last place this session, else darktable's default, as every `On` does, and Ctrl-click on the photo switches as ever. Its Restrict the scopes keeps it on when another tool's control changes or another tool's Show on photo turns on; collapsing its own section turns it off, as for every picker. Screen readers hear its face as a radio button, "Colour picker, Shift+K", checked while the picker is on, and "Colour picker on" and "Colour picker off" with the reason, as for every picker. Planned with the Colour picker section (79.16).
- **`PickerCommand::Once` stays** for scripts, the MCP and assistants; the app's cursors no longer send it (104.6).
- **The Layers section's mask and retouch buttons stay** (Phase 4) and share one state with the cursors: pressing a mask button chooses its cursor, which the well shows, and choosing the cursor shows the button on.
- **No "Set … from Here" items** in the photo's menu: a picker cursor's key turns the picker on at the area it holds, and a click or a drag there picks.

### The API

Cursors are the app's. The engine, the session protocol, the CLI and MCP never hear of them: a cursor turns a finished gesture into commands its owner already has.

- **In `apertrust-tools`, data only:** `CursorDef { id, group, icon, key, order, gestures, surfaces, options }`, `CursorGroup` (Select, Pan, Pick, ColourPicker, Mask, Retouch, Annotate, CopyApply, in the well's order; ColourPicker planned), `CursorGroupDef { group, settings }`, `Gesture` and `Surface`; and `ToolDefinition::cursors: &'static [ToolCursor]`, `ToolCursor { cursor, picker }`, a tool's picker cursor naming one of its pickers, with an icon of its own so the Pick face and the pointer's badge show which picker is on (`pick-white-balance`, `pick-exposure`), in Pick alone, as `menus` and `toolbar` are data ([0091](0091-context-menus-places-and-tools-items-as-actions.md), [0092](0092-the-toolbar.md), [0093](0093-one-tool-model-processing-tools-and-utility-tools.md)). A picker cursor declares no gestures of its own: its picker's kinds decide what a press does (planned, 104.6).
- **In the app:** `CursorInput` (a click, an area, a shape, a stroke, points, a photo, in the photo's coordinates with the keys held); one trait, `CursorOwner`, for every owner but a tool (the app's Select, Pan and Copy and apply; layers and masks; retouch; annotations): its cursors and group settings, whether a press can act now and why not, a gesture's live preview, its finished commands; one built-in owner for every tool's cursor; the Colour picker utility's cursor and its Start as through a `CursorOwner` of the app's Colour picker (planned, 79.16), since a `ToolCursor` names a tool's picker and a utility declares no cursors in its `UtilityDefinition`; the mode, `Cursor { owner, id }`, Select at every start, never saved.
- **A picker cursor sends its picker's `On` and `Off`** ([0079](0079-colour-pickers-one-api-for-tools-and-the-colour-picker.md)), with the tool's picker settings and the channel it shows, and the mode reads whether it is chosen from the mirror's `Picker` fact, so the two can never disagree; the presses on the photo are the picker's own commands, one step a drag, as decision 79 makes them.

## Options considered

- **"Set … from Here" items in the photo's right-click menu,** a one-shot pick at the point clicked: no way to pick by a drag, the pick hidden behind a right-click no touch or keyboard user makes first, two clicks a pick for a series, and nothing for masks, retouch or copying edits.
- **Each feature its own mode in its own place** (pickers in their tools, masks in the Layers section, annotations in a bar of their own): several modes that can clash over one press, no shared keys, and each new feature inventing how its mode is chosen and shown.
- **Every cursor its own segment in the well,** none behind a list: the well would reach about 386 points with Phase 4's cursors and fold, some cursors moving into a menu at the end, so a cursor's place would change with the window's width.
- **The well on the viewer bar:** the viewer bar will carry darktable's view toggles beside Apertrust's own, the layer menu, the values and the zoom, and would overflow at ordinary widths.
- **A help line on the viewer bar** saying what the chosen cursor does: text on screen all the time for what a tooltip says once.
- **A plate behind the chosen cursor:** a filled shape among the Toolbar's plate-free icons; the accent marks what a press on the photo acts with, as amber marks what is chosen elsewhere.
- **Cursors replacing the picker button,** a press on it choosing its cursor: one concept, but darktable's live picker, which the migration mirrors, would go.
- **A picker cursor picking once on its own,** each click or drag one `PickerCommand::Once`, chosen until another cursor is and across photos, beside the tool's live picker: one tool's picking with two lifetimes, two looks and two Undo rules, the well's accent showing one while the photo obeyed the other.
- **Both lifetimes kept,** with one indicator and one exit: the clash gone, but still two models to learn for one tool.
- **A sticky one-shot cursor that the picker button chooses:** one model, but a departure from darktable's live picker, whose readings follow the area as it moves.
- **The picker button alone, the picker cursors removed:** darktable's model, with no place on the Toolbar showing what a press on the photo does.
- **A picker kept on across photos:** a reading applied to every photo browsed, a silent edit.
- **The Pick face lit with a plain pipette** for a picker with no cursor of its own: a face the well cannot choose; every tool's picker gets its own cursor instead.
- **Opening and scrolling to the tool's section when its cursor is chosen,** as darktable focuses the module: the Tools panel moving for a quick action; the area's label and the pointer's badge say whose picker it is.
- **The photo's menu on a right-click while picking,** with a Reset Picker Area item: darktable's reset gone from the photo, while the mode is plain to see.
- **The picker button drawn in the accent while on:** one colour for one state, but the button's own On is decision 81's filled form; the accent stays the well's.
- **A system custom pointer per picker:** a bitmap at every scale and a way past egui's cursor setting, where the overlay's badge needs neither; worth it only if the badge's trailing frame shows in tests.
- **The Colour picker's cursor in Pick,** beside the tools' pickers: its picker writes nothing and reads the frame as shown, a different job from the tools', and Pick's face would switch between the two kinds; a group of its own keeps them apart for one face's width more.
- **I for the Colour picker's cursor,** the eyedropper's key in many editors: darktable holds I in the lighttable to show the photos' information; Shift+K is free in darktable's defaults and in Apertrust's map, and sits beside White balance's K.
- **A cursor ending after one use,** as a menu item's pick does: Copy and apply's series and a mask's strokes would need it chosen again each time.
- **The hand as the default:** a drag over a mark would pan where it should move the mark; Select moves a mark from a drag on it, and the pan is Pan's, A held's and the scroll's.
- **Select panning from a drag on empty photo,** darktable's drag-to-pan: Select and Pan would do the same on empty photo, and a drag meant for a mark that misses it would move the photo. Each cursor keeps one job; Pan, A held with any cursor, the scroll and the trackpad's swipe keep panning, and Ctrl+scroll and the pinch zooming.
- **Space for a held Pan:** Space is Next Photo; A is darktable's own key to force panning.
- **Cursors known to the engine:** a mode in the session for what is the interface's; every client already sends the commands a cursor sends.

## Consequences

- **[0079](0079-colour-pickers-one-api-for-tools-and-the-colour-picker.md):** a picker cursor sends `On` and `Off`, no new command; `PickerCommand::Once` and `Region::pressed` (built, 79.17) stay for scripts, the MCP and assistants. Its interface gains the well as a second door, the label on the area, the pointer's badge, Esc wherever the focus is, A held over a picker and the announced turn-offs; its focus rule becomes another tool's control changing, another tool's Show on photo turning on (planned) or its own tool's section collapsed, with no focused tool.
- **[0091](0091-context-menus-places-and-tools-items-as-actions.md):** its open question on an item that picks at the pointer is settled: no such item. While a picker is on, a right-click on the photo is the picker's.
- **[0092](0092-the-toolbar.md):** the part `cursors`, one, the whole well, in the default list between two flexible spaces, never folded; a hidden well leaves every cursor with a key reachable by it, and every cursor's work by its tool's own button.
- **[0098](0098-actions-and-shortcuts-after-darktable.md):** the cursor actions' family and kind, a picker cursor's `on` and `off` its picker's `On` and `Off`, and the default keys Esc, A held, K and Shift+K.
- **[0034](0034-layers-masked-tool-instances-in-a-fixed-order.md):** a mask's parts drawn from the Layers section's buttons or the Mask cursors, one state.
- **[0081](0081-icon-only-buttons-draw-no-plates-on-is-the-filled-icon.md):** a chosen cursor is shown by its icon in `accent_text`, not its filled form; the picker button keeps its filled pipette.
- **The built pan by a drag** is Pan's and A held's alone, a departure from darktable's drag-to-pan: Select's drag on empty photo does nothing. Pan's drag pans over marks too; the scroll and the trackpad's swipe pan, and Ctrl+scroll and the pinch zoom, with every cursor.
- **The code:** `apertrust-tools` gains the declarations and their checks, White balance's and Exposure's cursors first; `apertrust-ui` the well, in the gallery, and the overlay's badge, `Overlay::icon`, with the label's placement; the app the mode, the owners, the pointer shapes, the actions and keys and the well on the Toolbar. The built 104.4 picks once with `Once` and keeps Show the last pick (`cursors.pick.show_last`); 104.5 to 104.7 rework it: the well following the mirror's `Picker` fact, Esc, A held and the crosshair over a picker, and the announcements (104.5); the cursors sending `On` and `Off`, their faces disabled with the reason where the picker cannot turn on, Exposure's `reference` picker given its cursor, the check no longer refusing a cursor for a picker that writes no control, and the picker cursor's gestures, the last pick's mark and its setting gone, an old `show_last` key left as an unknown key (104.6); the label and the badge (104.7); the Colour picker's group, `CursorGroup::ColourPicker`, its cursor, icon, key and Start as, with the Colour picker section (104.8, with 79.16). The engine is unchanged. No new crate.
- **The docs:** [cursors.md](../cursors.md) holds the design and its open questions; pickers.md, gui.md, session-protocol.md, history.md, layers.md, crates.md and the roadmap follow it.
