# 0078. Undo, redo and the edit's history

## Context

[0047](0047-where-edits-live-and-what-the-sidecar-holds.md) made history a log of steps in the sidecar, Undo and Redo moving its end. It left the step's exact contents, what merges into one step, the commands and events clients use, how a client names who made a change, how the log is stored beside the library's step 8, and the order of the work. The clients are the app's Undo, Redo and History tab (the wireframe's History frame), `apertrust-cli`, the assistant and MCP, batch edits and styles, and later virtual copies, snapshots, several computers and the web client.

darktable 5.6.2, the reference ([0002](0002-rewrite-darktable-first-then-apertrusts-own-way.md)), keeps a history list with `history_end` and an undo stack in memory holding the whole history and its end before each change, a click on a history item and a compress among them (`src/common/undo.c`, `src/libs/history.c:664-686` and `:1297-1318`). Its history merges a module's consecutive changes (`_dev_add_history_item_ext`); its undo merges a widget's changes within `darkroom/undo/merge_same_secs` (10 s) unless `review_secs` (2 s) have passed since the pipe finished the last (`src/develop/develop.c:1351-1370`, `:885`).

## Decision

**One log per virtual copy in the sidecar, undo records in the session's memory as darktable's, and one `History` fact for every client of a session.** The design in full, with its types, the sidecar's layout, the behaviour, the CLI and the plan, is [history.md](../history.md). Planned, as the library's step 9.

- **A step** is one completed change (a gesture ending with `done`, a reset, a preset, a `Step`): a 64-bit id, a hybrid logical clock, a `StepSource` (You, Auto, Style, Assistant or Default), an optional note (a message id or an assistant's own text), the session's batch id, and each module it touched with its **whole stored state after it**, an empty entry being the module's reset state. The before is derived from the module's previous entry, never stored. An entry's key leaves room for a layer and an instance (Phase 4).
- **What merges:** darktable's undo rule, by target. A change of the same source, no note, on the target the step before changed (a control set or reset, a preset row applied, or a picker's pick, [0079](0079-colour-pickers-one-api-for-modules-and-the-colour-picker.md)), made just before it, within 10 s of that step's start and 2 s of the first frame published that includes its last change, merges into it.
- **Original** is end 0 and steps count from 1; the **Default lines** are the modules every photo runs, derived, never stored, unnumbered, never selected, a click opening the module.
- **Undo and Redo** take back and put back the session's undo records, as darktable's: a step added, which Undo takes out of the log, bringing back any steps it dropped; a `GoTo`; a `Compress` or a `Truncate`. So Undo takes back a click on a step, and after a click and an edit, which drops the later steps, it brings them back while the photo is open. The records live in the session's memory alone, a done stack and an undone stack, cleared as the photo closes. Only when the done stack is empty does Undo move the end back one step, and only when the undone stack is empty does Redo move it forward one; each such move is pushed as a `GoTo` record onto the opposite stack, so the next Redo or Undo reverses it, and clears no undone record. Each move of the end and each Compress or Truncate, or its Undo or Redo, advances the copy's clock vector with a clock of its own.
- **Reset is a step**, noted `history-reset`, turning switched modules off ([0032](0032-modules-off-until-first-touched.md)); Discard History is Reset. **Compress** is darktable's compress: each module's last entry up to the end kept, empty ones dropped, every step after the end dropped (`src/common/history.c:1244-1255`). **Truncate** is darktable's Ctrl-click on it: the steps after the end dropped, the rest kept as they are (`dt_history_truncate_on_image`, `src/common/history.c:1348-1399`, its delete `:1366-1376`); in the History tab, Ctrl-click (Cmd-click on the Mac) on Compress history, its tooltip saying so. Both are undoable while the photo is open and advance the copy's clock vector.
- **Commands,** all on the edit but the eye: `Undo`, `Redo`, `GoTo { step }`, `Compress`, `Truncate`, `Reset`, `Step { source, note, commands }` and `Hide { module, hidden }`, view state only: the eye shows the view as if the module were reset, off for a switched module and at its defaults for one every photo runs, whose operation is never skipped. `Step` takes only the edit's own commands, applies them to a scratch edit swapped in on success, is refused whole naming the failing index (`InStep { index, error }`), and refuses `Default`. The app sends its changes of several commands, the tone equalizer's release and band reset, as one `Step`. Batch ids are the session's.
- **One event:** `Event::History(History)`, a fact (`Fact::History`), whole each time, its steps shared behind `Arc`s: the steps with their changes before → after, the end, the Default lines and what Undo and Redo would do. Sent as a photo opens, once per batch that changed the history, after `Values` and before `Frame`, and when a newer file is taken from disk.
- **The sidecar:** version 2's copy holds its steps and `apertrust:end`, the last applied step's id; each entry as version 1 writes a module; version 1 reads as one step with an id hashed from its bytes. Its target is conformance to ISO 16684-1:2019 (which revises ISO 16684-1:2012, whose text Adobe publishes free as the XMP Specification Part 1), as the XMP layer's is ([0050](0050-our-own-xmp-layer.md)): every namespace declared with its URI, each step's time an XMP Date (`apertrust:when`, ISO 8601 with milliseconds in UTC) beside its counter and machine id, held by a fixture ExifTool and exiv2 read without error. No limit on steps, measured at 1,000. Switched modules ([0032](0032-modules-off-until-first-touched.md)) come before the log, as history relies on them throughout; step 8's clock, in `apertrust-library`, comes before the log too, and its version 2 with one virtual copy before history is saved.
- **The CLI** works on the sidecar outside any session, through the same engine functions: one step per `edit`, and `history` to list, undo, redo, go to a step, compress and truncate. The session takes a newer file before its own write, laying its unsaved steps on it.

## Options considered

- Undo as the end's move alone, with no records: one model in memory and on disk, but the steps an edit drops after a click on an old step are lost for good, at once, where darktable's Ctrl+Z brings them back.
- The eye skipping its module's operation: for White balance or the tone mapper it would show an image no edit can make.
- The clock as one text value (`2026-03-14T14:02:11.120Z 0 5e1f22c9`): no other tool would read the step's time as a date.
- darktable's undo stack as the only undo, history in memory: undo lost on closing, against [0047](0047-where-edits-live-and-what-the-sidecar-holds.md).
- No merging: the wheel or the arrow keys make dozens of steps where darktable's undo makes one. Merging as darktable's history items, by module until focus moves: one Undo would take back a whole run on a module.
- Steps storing a delta, or before and after: a before stored twice, and deltas cannot migrate one at a time.
- A history event of deltas: against [0059](0059-one-event-system.md)'s whole facts, needing a replay for `Sync` and a merge in every client, for a topic smaller than a frame.
- Source and note on every edit command, or a session-wide current source: every command grows, a request on several controls still needs grouping, and two clients in one session would race.
- A client's batch id: clients could collide, and a batch must be one command.
- Compress and Truncate that cannot be undone: against principle 3's "history and undo cover everything" and darktable.
- Truncate as a flag of Compress (`Compress { truncate }`): two operations of different effect under one name, where a command each reads beside `Undo`, `Redo` and `GoTo`.
- History in a version 2 of its own before step 8: moving it into `apertrust:copies` later would change how existing files read, against [0048](0048-the-apxmp-format.md)'s one fixed layout.
- Default steps stored in the log: they would duplicate defaults that module versions carry ([0048](0048-the-apxmp-format.md)).
- The end stored as a count: it shifts when two logs join by clock (step 14). 32-bit step ids: a collision becomes likely across many joined logs.

## Consequences

- The engine gains `history`; the session its commands, undo records and `Event::History`; the library's sidecar version 2's copies and steps; the app Undo, Redo, the History tab and its changes of several commands as one `Step`; the CLI `edit`'s step and `history`.
- [0047](0047-where-edits-live-and-what-the-sidecar-holds.md) names a step's source and note, the end's id and the clock vector's moves; [0052](0052-comparing-two-copies-of-a-sidecar.md) compares each copy's latest clock, its clock vector included; [0031](0031-views-and-curves-of-the-same-stored-values.md) the tone equalizer's release as one `Step`; [0050](0050-our-own-xmp-layer.md) cites ISO 16684-1:2019 and gains a fixture with a history; the roadmap's step 9 lists its pull requests, 9.1 to 9.9, with step 8's parts 8.1 and 8.2 where history needs them.
- Auto, styles, batches, the assistant, snapshots, virtual copies, diverged logs and layers each add their steps when built, through `Step` and the entries' key; a picker's pick is a change the session makes itself, of source Auto ([0079](0079-colour-pickers-one-api-for-modules-and-the-colour-picker.md)).
