# 0078. Undo, redo and the edit's history

## Context

[0047](0047-where-edits-live-and-what-the-sidecar-holds.md) made history a log of steps in the sidecar, Undo and Redo moving its end. It left the step's exact contents, what merges into one step, the commands and events clients use, how a client names who made a change, how the log is stored beside the library's step 8, and the order of the work. The clients are the app's Undo, Redo and History tab (the wireframe's History frame), `apertrust-cli`, the assistant and MCP, batch edits and styles, and later virtual copies, snapshots, several computers and the web client.

darktable 5.6.2, the reference ([0002](0002-rewrite-darktable-first-then-apertrusts-own-way.md)), keeps a history list with `history_end` and an undo stack in memory holding the whole history and its end before each change, a click on a history item and a compress among them (`src/common/undo.c`, `src/libs/history.c:664-686` and `:1297-1318`). Its history item is exactly one module, its parameters, blend parameters and on/off (`dt_dev_history_item_t`, `src/develop/develop.h:34`); applying a style, or copying or pasting history, adds one item per module, wrapped in one undo group (`dt_undo_start_group`, `src/common/styles.c:954`, `src/common/history.c:157`, `:200`, `:229`, `:971`), so one Ctrl+Z takes the whole group back while the history lists each module. Its history merges a module's consecutive changes (`_dev_add_history_item_ext`); its undo merges a widget's changes within `darkroom/undo/merge_same_secs` (10 s) unless `review_secs` (2 s) have passed since the pipe finished the last (`src/develop/develop.c:1351-1370`, `:885`).

## Decision

**One log per virtual copy in the sidecar, undo records in the session's memory as darktable's, and one `History` fact for every client of a session.** The design in full, with its types, the sidecar's layout, the behaviour, the CLI and the plan, is [history.md](../history.md). Planned, as the library's step 9.

- **A step is exactly one module's change,** or one unowned operation's parameters, as darktable's history item: a 64-bit id, a hybrid logical clock, a `StepSource` (You, Auto, Style, Assistant or Default), an optional note (a message id or an assistant's own text), an optional batch id, and the module with its **whole stored state after it**, an empty entry being the module's reset state. The before is derived from the module's previous entry, never stored. An entry's key leaves room for a layer and an instance (Phase 4).
- **A completed change touching several modules** (a preset row writing several, a `Step` whose commands write several; later styles and assistants) is a step of each, in the order its commands wrote them, each with the change's source and note, sharing one batch id, the session's, as darktable's undo group. A module the change touched that no command named, as one module turning another off, comes after them.
- **What merges:** darktable's undo rule, by target. A change of one module, of the same source, no note, on the target the step before changed (a control set or reset, a preset row applied, or a picker's pick, [0079](0079-colour-pickers-one-api-for-modules-and-the-colour-picker.md)), made just before it, within 10 s of that step's start and 2 s of the first frame published that includes its last change, merges into it. A step of a batch never merges and is never merged into.
- **Original** is end 0 and steps count from 1; the **Default lines** are the modules every photo runs, derived, never stored, unnumbered and never greyed: a click on one goes to Original (`GoTo { step: None }`, nothing sent at end 0), Shift-click opens its module, at end 0 the top Default line is highlighted as the end, and they have no eye.
- **Undo and Redo** take back and put back the session's undo records, as darktable's: one change's steps added, a batch's whole as darktable's undo group, which Undo takes out of the log, bringing back any steps they dropped; a `GoTo`; a `Compress` or a `Truncate`. So Undo takes back a click on a step, and after a click and an edit, which drops the later steps, it brings them back while the photo is open. The records live in the session's memory alone, a done stack and an undone stack, cleared as the photo closes. Only when the done stack is empty does Undo move the end back over the step before it and the rest of its batch, and only when the undone stack is empty does Redo move it forward over the next step and the rest of its batch; each such move is pushed onto the opposite stack as a record of the steps it crosses, named `Move::Steps`, so the next Redo or Undo reverses it, and clears no undone record. `GoTo` lands on any step, one inside a batch among them, as darktable's click on any item. Each move of the end and each Compress or Truncate, or its Undo or Redo, advances the copy's clock vector with a clock of its own.
- **Reset is the one exception, one step**, noted `history-reset`, holding an empty entry for every module it reset, so the History tab shows one Reset line, turning switched modules off ([0032](0032-modules-off-until-first-touched.md)), the log kept: the top panel's Reset, Edit › Reset Edit and the History header's ↺.
- **Discard History…** is a command of its own, `DiscardHistory`, darktable's discard: it clears the history and resets the edit, doing what going to Original and then Compress does, since Compress at end 0 deletes the whole log, as one undo record that takes it back while the photo is open. It asks first, with Don't ask again (darktable's `ask_before_discard`). It lives in Edit › Discard History… and the History section's ⋯ options menu, never as a button in the History header nor in the photo's right-click menu. Planned with 9.8.
  - It is the one command allowed on an edit open for viewing only because of a `sidecar::Problem` ([0048](0048-the-apxmp-format.md)), never on one a newer build wrote. Warning first, so the person can keep a copy of the file, it writes a fresh log holding a step of each module the edit holds as far as it was read, sharing a batch, source You, noted `history-discard`, the end the last, and ends the view-only state. Original stays the untouched photo.
- **Compress** is darktable's compress: each module's last entry up to the end kept, empty ones dropped, every step after the end dropped (`src/common/history.c:1244-1255`). **Truncate** is darktable's Ctrl-click on it: the steps after the end dropped, the rest kept as they are (`dt_history_truncate_on_image`, `src/common/history.c:1348-1399`, its delete `:1366-1376`); in the History tab, Ctrl-click (Cmd-click on the Mac) on Compress history, its tooltip saying so. Both are undoable while the photo is open and advance the copy's clock vector.
- **Commands,** all on the edit but the eye: `Undo`, `Redo`, `GoTo { step }`, `Compress`, `Truncate`, `Reset`, `DiscardHistory` (planned with 9.8), `Step { source, note, commands }` and `Hide { module, hidden }`, view state only: the eye shows the view as if the module were reset, off for a switched module and at its defaults for one every photo runs, whose operation is never skipped. `Step` takes only the edit's own commands and applies them in place, in order, the edit, its held coordinates and whether the writer has it put back should one be refused; it is refused whole naming the failing index (`InStep { index, error }`), and refuses `Default` and a `Step` holding nothing before it touches the gesture under way. A gesture has one source: only a change of its source and note goes on with it or takes it in. The app sends its changes of several commands, the tone equalizer's release and band reset, each on its one module, as one `Step`. Batch ids are the session's.
- **One event:** `Event::History(History)`, a fact (`Fact::History`), whole each time, its steps shared behind `Arc`s: the steps with their batch ids and changes before → after, the end, the Default lines and what Undo and Redo would do, the steps of one change, a batch's whole. Sent as a photo opens, once per batch that changed the history, after `Values` and before `Frame`, and when a newer file is taken from disk.
- **The sidecar:** version 2's copy holds its steps and `apertrust:end`, the last applied step's id; each entry as version 1 writes a module; version 1 reads as a step of each module, sharing a batch, with ids hashed from its bytes. Its target is conformance to ISO 16684-1:2019 (which revises ISO 16684-1:2012, whose text Adobe publishes free as the XMP Specification Part 1), as the XMP layer's is ([0050](0050-our-own-xmp-layer.md)): every namespace declared with its URI, each step's time an XMP Date (`apertrust:when`, ISO 8601 with milliseconds in UTC) beside its counter and machine id, held by a fixture ExifTool and exiv2 read without error. No limit on steps, measured at 1,000. Switched modules ([0032](0032-modules-off-until-first-touched.md)) come before the log, as history relies on them throughout; step 8's clock, in `apertrust-library`, comes before the log too, and its version 2 with one virtual copy before history is saved.
- **The CLI** works on the sidecar outside any session, through the same engine functions: one change per `edit`, a step of each module its flags write, sharing a batch, and `history` to list, undo, redo, go to a step, compress and truncate. The session takes a newer file before its own write, laying its unsaved steps on it.

## Options considered

- One step holding several modules, a change's or a style's: it is not darktable's history, whose items are one module each, grouped only in undo, so a style or a paste would read as one line where darktable lists each module.
- Undo as the end's move alone, with no records: one model in memory and on disk, but the steps an edit drops after a click on an old step are lost for good, at once, where darktable's Ctrl+Z brings them back.
- The eye skipping its module's operation: for White balance or the tone mapper it would show an image no edit can make.
- The clock as one text value (`2026-03-14T14:02:11.120Z 0 5e1f22c9`): no other tool would read the step's time as a date.
- darktable's undo stack as the only undo, history in memory: undo lost on closing, against [0047](0047-where-edits-live-and-what-the-sidecar-holds.md).
- No merging: the wheel or the arrow keys make dozens of steps where darktable's undo makes one. Merging as darktable's history items, by module until focus moves: one Undo would take back a whole run on a module.
- Steps storing a delta, or before and after: a before stored twice, and deltas cannot migrate one at a time.
- A history event of deltas: against [0059](0059-one-event-system.md)'s whole facts, needing a replay for `Sync` and a merge in every client, for a topic smaller than a frame.
- Source and note on every edit command, or a session-wide current source: every command grows, a request on several controls still needs grouping, and two clients in one session would race.
- A client's batch id: clients could collide, and a batch must be one command.
- Discard History as Reset, one step with the log kept: darktable's discard clears the history, and an edit view-only because part of its history cannot be read needs a way to start the log afresh.
- Compress and Truncate that cannot be undone: against principle 3's "history and undo cover everything" and darktable.
- Truncate as a flag of Compress (`Compress { truncate }`): two operations of different effect under one name, where a command each reads beside `Undo`, `Redo` and `GoTo`.
- History in a version 2 of its own before step 8: moving it into `apertrust:copies` later would change how existing files read, against [0048](0048-the-apxmp-format.md)'s one fixed layout.
- Default steps stored in the log: they would duplicate defaults that module versions carry ([0048](0048-the-apxmp-format.md)).
- The end stored as a count: it shifts when two logs join by clock (step 14). 32-bit step ids: a collision becomes likely across many joined logs.

## Consequences

- The engine gains `history`; the session its commands, undo records and `Event::History`; the library's sidecar version 2's copies and steps; the app Undo, Redo, the History tab and its changes of several commands as one `Step`; the CLI `edit`'s step and `history`.
- [0047](0047-where-edits-live-and-what-the-sidecar-holds.md) names a step's source and note, the end's id and the clock vector's moves; [0052](0052-comparing-two-copies-of-a-sidecar.md) compares each copy's latest clock, its clock vector included; [0031](0031-views-and-curves-of-the-same-stored-values.md) the tone equalizer's release as one `Step`; [0050](0050-our-own-xmp-layer.md) cites ISO 16684-1:2019 and gains a fixture with a history; the roadmap's step 9 lists its pull requests, 9.1 to 9.9, with step 8's parts 8.1 and 8.2 where history needs them.
- Auto, styles, batches, the assistant, snapshots, virtual copies, diverged logs and layers each add their steps when built, through `Step` and the entries' key; a picker's pick is a change the session makes itself, of source Auto ([0079](0079-colour-pickers-one-api-for-modules-and-the-colour-picker.md)).
