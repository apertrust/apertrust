# 0078. Undo, redo and the edit's history

## Context

[0047](0047-where-edits-live-and-what-the-sidecar-holds.md) made history a log of steps in the sidecar, Undo and Redo moving its end. It left the step's exact contents, what merges into one step, the commands and events clients use, how a client names who made a change, how the log is stored beside the library's step 8, and the order of the work. The clients are the app's Undo, Redo and History tab (the wireframe's History frame), `apertrust-cli`, the assistant and MCP, batch edits and styles, and later virtual copies, snapshots, several computers and the web client.

darktable 5.6.2, the reference ([0002](0002-rewrite-darktable-first-then-apertrusts-own-way.md)), keeps a history list with `history_end` and an undo stack in memory holding the whole history and its end before each change, a click on a history item and a compress among them (`src/common/undo.c`, `src/libs/history.c:664-686` and `:1297-1318`). Its history item is exactly one module, its parameters, blend parameters and on/off (`dt_dev_history_item_t`, `src/develop/develop.h:34`); applying a style, or copying or pasting history, adds one item per module, wrapped in one undo group (`dt_undo_start_group`, `src/common/styles.c:954`, `src/common/history.c:157`, `:200`, `:229`, `:971`), so one Ctrl+Z takes the whole group back while the history lists each module. Its history merges a module's consecutive changes (`_dev_add_history_item_ext`); its undo merges a widget's changes within `darkroom/undo/merge_same_secs` (10 s) unless `review_secs` (2 s) have passed since the pipe finished the last (`src/develop/develop.c:1351-1370`, `:885`).

## Decision

**One log per virtual copy in the sidecar, undo records in the session's memory as darktable's, and one `History` fact for every client of a session.** The design in full, with its types, the sidecar's layout, the behaviour, the CLI and the plan, is [history.md](../history.md). Planned, as the library's step 9.

- **A step is exactly one module's change,** or one unowned operation's parameters, as darktable's history item: a 64-bit id, a hybrid logical clock, a `StepSource` (You, Auto, Style, Assistant or Default), an optional note (a message id or an assistant's own text), an optional batch id, and the module with its **whole stored state after it**, an empty entry being the module's reset state. The before is derived from the module's previous entry, never stored. An entry's key leaves room for a layer and an instance (Phase 4).
- **A completed change touching several modules** (a preset row writing several, a `Step` whose commands write several; later styles and assistants) is a step of each, in the order its commands wrote them, each with the change's source and note, sharing one batch id, the session's, as darktable's undo group. A module the change touched that no command named, as one module turning another off, comes after them. The History tab shows a batch as one closed group, its note or how many modules, its range of numbers and its modules, opening to its steps, a line a module; a click on its line goes to its last step, as Undo moves it whole.
- **What merges:** darktable's undo rule, by target. A change of one module, of the same source, no note, on the target the step before changed (a control set or reset, a preset row applied, or a picker's pick, [0079](0079-colour-pickers-one-api-for-modules-and-the-colour-picker.md)), made just before it, within 10 s of that step's start and 2 s of the first frame published that includes its last change, merges into it. A step of a batch never merges and is never merged into.
- **Original** is end 0 and steps count from 1.
- **A photo opened with no history is given its default steps,** as darktable's `dt_dev_read_history_ext` gives a new raw its default modules and its workflow's auto-applied presets: a step of source Default of each of darktable's default modules, at its reset state, in darktable's history order for a new raw: the modules it has on by default with no on/off button (Raw black/white point, Demosaic, Output colour profile), then those with one (White balance, Highlight reconstruction, Orientation), then those its default scene-referred workflow auto-applies (Exposure, Colour calibration and the tone mapper, Apertrust's AgX), each group in the pipe's order; steps 1 to n. Any other module, Tone curve among them, which no preset applies, runs at its defaults with no step. Opening writes nothing, nor does a move among them before the first change, which writes them with it; once the log is in the file, a move among them is saved as any move of the end.
  - **Stable numbers:** a history that has steps is given none. A default module a later build adds runs at its defaults with no step, and the steps keep their numbers.
  - **Version 1 and any history with steps but no default steps** are read as they are, with no default step added, Original showing every module at its defaults, as darktable applies its auto-applied presets once in a history's lifetime (`DT_IMAGE_AUTO_PRESETS_APPLIED`, `_dev_auto_apply_presets`); one rule keeps every history's numbers.
  - **Undo's fallback stops above them,** as darktable's auto-applied items are no undo records; `GoTo` reaches 0 to n.
  - **The end before a default step** leaves its module not applied where darktable leaves that module off until its workflow applies it, Exposure, Colour calibration and the tone mapper: its operations are left out of the plan and it is reported off, as darktable's photo below its auto-applied items shows. A module darktable has on by default runs at its defaults below its step, as darktable's `dt_dev_pop_history_items_ext` resets it to `default_enabled`. So a click on step k shows the photo with the first k applied. It is the history's state at that step, never an edit turning a module off ([0032](0032-modules-off-until-first-touched.md)); every reader of the file folds it from the log at its saved end, so thumbnails and exports render what the view shows.
  - **The first change with the end among them,** a Reset, a module's reset or a module's picker turned on among them, keeps every default step after the end, moved below it before the change's steps, and applies them all; Reset, a module's reset and a module's picker turned on there are one step each though they move no value, and Reset is available there though the edit holds nothing; an override `apertrust-cli` applies to a saved edit there is such a change too. Its Undo puts the default steps back after the end. This differs from darktable on purpose: darktable's `_dev_add_history_item_ext` keeps above the end only an always-on module's item with no earlier entry and drops the auto-applied items of Exposure, Colour calibration and the tone mapper, which are not on by default and have an on/off button (`src/develop/develop.c:1215-1222`), so its photo goes on without them; Apertrust keeps every default step, the modules every photo runs never being off (0032). darktable also keeps a person's later item of an always-on module with no earlier entry, where Apertrust keeps only the default steps.
  - **The History tab** numbers them 1 to n, Original 0, as one closed group, Defaults, over Original, opening as a batch's does; a click on its line goes to its last step, a click on one inside it to that step, and Shift-click opens its module, as on any step.
- **Undo and Redo** take back and put back the session's undo records, as darktable's: one change's steps added, a batch's whole as darktable's undo group, which Undo takes out of the log, bringing back any steps they dropped; a `GoTo`; a `Compress`, a `Truncate` or a `DiscardHistory`, the log as it was. So Undo takes back a click on a step, and after a click and an edit, which drops the later steps, it brings them back while the photo is open. The records live in the session's memory alone, a done stack and an undone stack, cleared as the photo closes. Only when the done stack is empty does Undo move the end back over the step before it and the rest of its batch, and only when the undone stack is empty does Redo move it forward over the next step and the rest of its batch; each such move is pushed onto the opposite stack as a record of the steps it crosses, named `Move::Steps`, so the next Redo or Undo reverses it, and clears no undone record. `GoTo` lands on any step, one inside a batch among them, as darktable's click on any item. Each move of the end and each Compress or Truncate, or its Undo or Redo, advances the copy's clock vector with a clock of its own.
- **Reset is the one exception, one step**, noted `history-reset`, holding an empty entry for every module it reset, so the History tab shows one Reset line, turning switched modules off ([0032](0032-modules-off-until-first-touched.md)), the log kept: the top panel's Reset, Edit › Reset Edit and the History header's ↺.
- **Discard History…** is a command of its own, `DiscardHistory`, darktable's discard: it clears the history and resets the edit, doing what going to Original and then Compress does, since Compress at end 0 deletes the whole log, as one undo record that takes it back while the photo is open. It asks first, with Don't ask again (darktable's `ask_before_discard`, kept as `history.ask_before_discard`). It lives in Edit › Discard History… and the History section's ⋯ options menu, never as a button in the History header nor in the photo's right-click menu.
  - It is the one command allowed on an edit open for viewing only because of a `sidecar::Problem` ([0048](0048-the-apxmp-format.md)), never on one a newer build wrote. Warning first, so the person can keep a copy of the file, it writes a fresh log, what the file held that could not be read going with the old one, and ends the view-only state. The fresh log is a new photo's default steps, then a step of each module the edit holds as far as it was read, sharing a batch, source You, noted `history-discard`, the end the last; the default steps of the modules not applied in the edit as read come after the end, so nothing changes on screen, and Original stays the untouched photo.
- **Compress** is darktable's compress: each module's last entry up to the end kept, empty ones dropped, the person's steps after the end dropped (`src/common/history.c:1244-1255`), and every default step kept, wherever the end, a later step of its module following or not. **Truncate** is darktable's Ctrl-click on it: the person's steps after the end dropped, the rest kept as they are, the default steps after the end among them (`dt_history_truncate_on_image`, `src/common/history.c:1348-1399`, its delete `:1366-1376`). Both live in the History section's ⋯ options menu, with no buttons under the list: Compress History, which Option held while the menu is open (Alt on Windows and Linux) turns into Truncate History, as macOS's alternate menu items do, offered where a step after the end is not a default one, its tip saying so. So neither changes what the photo shows, at the end or at Original, as darktable keeps its auto-applied flag; only at end 0 do they drop every step, as darktable deletes the whole history, and the photo, with no history, is given its default steps again. Keeping every default step differs from darktable, whose compress needs no item for a module at its default: the default steps are what Original and the Defaults group show. Both are undoable while the photo is open and advance the copy's clock vector.
- **Commands,** all on the edit but the eye: `Undo`, `Redo`, `GoTo { step }`, `Compress`, `Truncate`, `Reset`, `DiscardHistory`, `Step { source, note, commands }` and `Hide { module, hidden }`, view state only: the eye, held, shows the view as if the module were reset, off for a switched module and at its defaults for one every photo runs, whose operation is never skipped, a default module the end leaves not applied staying out; a momentary peek, never a toggle, the app sending `hidden` true as it is pressed and false as it is let go, and nothing kept once the photo closes or another opens. While any module is hidden, a module's colour picker measures nothing, its reading waiting until all are shown, and `Picker` `On` for one is refused (`Hidden`), since it would read the view and write the edit ([0079](0079-colour-pickers-one-api-for-modules-and-the-colour-picker.md)); the Colour picker module's picker, which reads the frame shown and writes nothing, is untouched. `Step` takes only the edit's own commands and applies them in place, in order, the edit, its held coordinates and whether the writer has it put back should one be refused; it is refused whole naming the failing index (`InStep { index, error }`), and refuses `Default` and a `Step` holding nothing before it touches the gesture under way. A gesture has one source: only a change of its source and note goes on with it or takes it in. The app sends its changes of several commands, the tone equalizer's release and band reset, each on its one module, as one `Step`. Batch ids are the session's.
- **One event:** `Event::History(History)`, a fact (`Fact::History`), whole each time, its steps shared behind `Arc`s: the steps with their batch ids and changes before → after, the default steps among them, the end and what Undo and Redo would do, the steps of one change, a batch's whole. Sent as a photo opens, once per batch that changed the history, after `Values` and before `Frame`, and when a newer file is taken from disk.
- **The sidecar:** version 2's copy holds its steps and `apertrust:end`, the last applied step's id; each entry as version 1 writes a module; version 1 reads as a step of each module, sharing a batch, with ids hashed from its bytes. Its target is conformance to ISO 16684-1:2019 (which revises ISO 16684-1:2012, whose text Adobe publishes free as the XMP Specification Part 1), as the XMP layer's is ([0050](0050-our-own-xmp-layer.md)): every namespace declared with its URI, each step's time an XMP Date (`apertrust:when`, ISO 8601 with milliseconds in UTC) beside its counter and machine id, held by a fixture ExifTool and exiv2 read without error. No limit on steps, measured at 1,000. Switched modules ([0032](0032-modules-off-until-first-touched.md)) come before the log, as history relies on them throughout; step 8's clock, in `apertrust-library`, comes before the log too, and its version 2 with one virtual copy before history is saved.
- **The CLI** works on the sidecar outside any session, through the same engine functions: one change per `edit`, a step of each module its flags write, sharing a batch, and `history` to list, undo, redo, go to a step, compress and truncate. The session takes a newer file before its own write, laying its unsaved steps on it.

## Options considered

- Compress History as a button under the list, Ctrl-click (Cmd-click on the Mac) truncating, as darktable's: a modifier on a click has no menu or screen-reader equivalent, and the list keeps its room; the ⋯ menu's alternate item is the platform's own form of the same choice.
- Compress keeping only a default step that is its module's last, as darktable's compress keeps the auto-applied item: Original and a default step's photo would then show such a module at its defaults rather than not applied, and the Defaults group would lose lines.
- Compress and Truncate dropping the default steps after the end, as darktable deletes the items above it: Exposure, Colour calibration and the tone mapper would then apply, changing the photo for good.
- One step holding several modules, a change's or a style's: it is not darktable's history, whose items are one module each, grouped only in undo, so a style or a paste would read as one line with nothing under it, where darktable lists each module and a batch's group opens to them.
- Undo as the end's move alone, with no records: one model in memory and on disk, but the steps an edit drops after a click on an old step are lost for good, at once, where darktable's Ctrl+Z brings them back.
- The eye skipping its module's operation: for White balance or the tone mapper it would show an image no edit can make.
- The eye as a toggle, a module left hidden until shown again: a slider of the hidden module then seems to do nothing.
- A module's picker turned off as a module is hidden: a momentary peek would end the picker the person was using.
- A module's picker sampling the real edit's plan while a module is hidden: a second pipe rendered beside the view for the peek's moment.
- The clock as one text value (`2026-03-14T14:02:11.120Z 0 5e1f22c9`): no other tool would read the step's time as a date.
- darktable's undo stack as the only undo, history in memory: undo lost on closing, against [0047](0047-where-edits-live-and-what-the-sidecar-holds.md).
- No merging: the wheel or the arrow keys make dozens of steps where darktable's undo makes one. Merging as darktable's history items, by module until focus moves: one Undo would take back a whole run on a module.
- Steps storing a delta, or before and after: a before stored twice, and deltas cannot migrate one at a time.
- A history event of deltas: against [0059](0059-one-event-system.md)'s whole facts, needing a replay for `Sync` and a merge in every client, for a topic smaller than a frame.
- Source and note on every edit command, or a session-wide current source: every command grows, a request on several controls still needs grouping, and two clients in one session would race.
- A client's batch id: clients could collide, and a batch must be one command.
- Discard History as Reset, one step with the log kept: darktable's discard clears the history, and an edit view-only because part of its history cannot be read needs a way to start the log afresh.
- Compress and Truncate that cannot be undone: against principle 3's "history and undo cover everything" and darktable.
- Truncate as a flag of Compress (`Compress { truncate }`): two operations of different effect under one name, where a command each reads beside `Undo`, `Redo` and `GoTo`.
- History in a version 2 of its own before step 8: moving it into `apertrust:copies` later would change how existing files read, against [0048](0048-the-apxmp-format.md)'s one fixed layout.
- The modules every photo runs as Default lines, derived, never stored and unnumbered: darktable numbers its default and auto-applied items, and a click on one goes there; without them as steps no step shows the photo before them.
- Default steps holding each module's defaults for the photo: they would duplicate defaults that module versions carry ([0048](0048-the-apxmp-format.md)); an empty entry is the module's reset state already.
- Default steps for every module every photo runs, Tone curve among them: no preset of darktable's applies Tone curve, so its history has no item of it.
- A default step added to a history that has steps, for a default module a later build adds, as darktable's `_dev_add_default_modules` prepends a default-enabled module missing from any history it reads: every existing step's number would shift.
- Undo moving over the default steps: darktable's auto-applied items are no undo records.
- The end below a default step changing nothing, every module at its defaults: a click on a default step would show nothing of it.
- A change with the end among them keeping only the default steps of modules darktable has on by default, dropping the rest, as darktable does: Exposure, Colour calibration and the tone mapper would still run, the modules every photo runs never being off (0032), with no step to show them.
- The end stored as a count: it shifts when two logs join by clock (step 14). 32-bit step ids: a collision becomes likely across many joined logs.

## Consequences

- The engine gains `history`; the session its commands, undo records and `Event::History`; the library's sidecar version 2's copies and steps; the app Undo, Redo, the History tab and its changes of several commands as one `Step`; the CLI `edit`'s step and `history`.
- The default steps: the log gives a new photo's, the session as the photo opens; the edit holds the modules not applied, which `edit::instances` leaves out and the values report off; the saved log (9.5) holds them from the first change, never before, and its readers fold the modules not applied from its end; Compress and Truncate (9.8) keep every default step, so they never change what the photo shows but at end 0; the CLI's first `edit` of a photo with no history (9.9) writes them before its change; `DiscardHistory` (9.8) leaves a photo with no history, given its default steps again, as darktable clears its auto-applied flag with the history (`src/common/history.c:54`).
- [0047](0047-where-edits-live-and-what-the-sidecar-holds.md) names a step's source and note, the end's id and the clock vector's moves; [0052](0052-comparing-two-copies-of-a-sidecar.md) compares each copy's latest clock, its clock vector included; [0031](0031-views-and-curves-of-the-same-stored-values.md) the tone equalizer's release as one `Step`; [0050](0050-our-own-xmp-layer.md) cites ISO 16684-1:2019 and gains a fixture with a history; the roadmap's step 9 lists its pull requests, 9.1 to 9.9, with step 8's parts 8.1 and 8.2 where history needs them.
- Auto, styles, batches, the assistant, snapshots, virtual copies, diverged logs and layers each add their steps when built, through `Step` and the entries' key; a picker's pick is a change the session makes itself, of source Auto ([0079](0079-colour-pickers-one-api-for-modules-and-the-colour-picker.md)).
