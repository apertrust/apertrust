# History and undo

Every change to a photo's edit makes steps of its history, one for each module it changed, as darktable's history lists one item per module: a slider's drag, a preset, a reset, an assistant's request. Undo and Redo move through those steps, the History tab lists them, and the sidecar keeps them, so reopening a photo brings Undo back. This document describes the history: what a step is, how the session serves undo and history to its clients, how the sidecar stores them, and how the work is built. Where edits live and the sidecar's other parts are in [library.md](library.md#sidecars); the session's other commands and events in [session-protocol.md](session-protocol.md) and [events.md](events.md).

**Status.** [Decision 78](decisions/0078-undo-redo-and-the-edits-history.md) records the design, with the options considered, on [decision 47](decisions/0047-where-edits-live-and-what-the-sidecar-holds.md)'s log of steps. Built, as step 9.1: the log in the session's memory, each completed change a step of each module it touched, several sharing a batch id that Undo and Redo move whole, `Reset` one step, `Undo` and `Redo` with the undo records, and `Event::History`; as step 9.2: `GoTo` with its undo record, `Step` with its refusals and the gesture taken in, and the app sending the tone equalizer's release of several bands and its reset of the nine as one `Step { source: You }`; as step 9.3: merging consecutive changes by target, darktable's 10 s and 2 s, timed from when each command was sent and each frame published; as step 9.4: Edit › Undo and Redo and their keys in the app, enabled from the mirror's `History` and naming what they would do; as step 9.6: the History tab's list, a click going to a step or Original, Shift-click opening modules, Reset in its header, and a module this build does not know listed by its stored name, then what each line says changed, and a batch as a closed group, day headings, a tree's keys and the header staying at the tab's top; as step 8.2: version 2's text layer, `sidecar::Edits`, with its fixture, and a version 1 file read through it as a step of each module, sharing a batch; as step 9.5: the log saved in the sidecar's version 2 with its end and the copy's clock vector, so reopening a photo shows every step and Undo and Redo go on through their fallback, a newer file taken in before a write, an edit with a `sidecar::Problem` open for viewing only with a caption of its own, and `apertrust-cli edit` adding a step of each module it writes; and the default steps: a photo with no history given a default step of each of darktable's default modules, written with the first change, Undo stopping above them, `GoTo` one showing the photo with the first k applied, the History tab's Defaults group numbering them ([Original and the default steps](#original-and-the-default-steps)); as step 9.7: the eye, `Hide` and `ModuleState::hidden`, the live frame showing a module as if reset, and the History tab's eye, held for a peek at the photo without a module; as step 9.8's engine half: `Compress`, `Truncate` and `DiscardHistory` with their undo records, a log they leave with no step given the default steps again, the file then written with none, and `DiscardHistory` starting afresh an edit open for viewing only because of a `sidecar::Problem`. Not built: the app's half of 9.8 (Compress history in the History tab, Discard History… and their words), `edit --note` and the CLI's history. The rest of the library's step 9 follows ([The plan](#the-plan)).

## What it serves

| Client | What it needs |
| --- | --- |
| The desktop app | Edit › Undo and Redo and their keys, naming the step ("Undo Exposure"); the History tab; Reset in its header as a step |
| `apertrust-cli` | `edit` adding one change, a step of each module it writes; `history` to list, undo, redo, go to a step, compress and truncate |
| The assistant and the MCP server | One undoable step per request, carrying its source and a note on what it did (principle 8; [ai.md](ai.md)) |
| Batch edits | Apply Edit to the selection and styles: a step of each module on each photo, sharing a batch id, one Undo for all (decision 37) |
| Later | Virtual copies and snapshots (the library's steps 11 and 16), several computers (steps 8 and 12), the web client over the session protocol (decision 58) |

Every client of a session, the app, MCP and later the web client, uses the same commands and draws the same `History` fact. The CLI's `edit` and `history` work on the sidecar outside any session, through the same engine functions ([The CLI](#the-cli)).

## The log

Each virtual copy of a photo has one log: its steps in order, and its **end**, how many of them apply, darktable's `history_end`. End 0 is Original.

**The edit is folded from the log:** for each module, its entry at its last step among the first `end`, else its reset state, its defaults for the photo and off when it is switched ([decision 32](decisions/0032-modules-off-until-first-touched.md)). Nothing else stores the edit; the session folds it as a photo opens and keeps it in step with each change, and export, thumbnails and the index read the same fold.

The log is the engine's (`apertrust_engine::history`): `Log`, `Step` and the functions that add, fold, merge, compress (`Log::compressed`), truncate (`Log::truncated`) and start an unreadable history afresh (`Log::afresh`), plain data, used by the session and, planned, by `apertrust-cli`. The sidecar's text layer (`apertrust_library::sidecar`, [decision 54](decisions/0054-four-layers-for-the-librarys-code.md)) holds the steps as strings, knowing no module's meaning.

## A step

### Its fields

| Field | Type | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | `StepId`, 16 hex digits | Random, 64 bits, so two logs joined step by step (step 14) do not collide; drawn again on a clash within its log |
| `clock` | `Clock` | The hybrid logical clock of its last change: UTC milliseconds, a counter and the machine id (decision 47; `apertrust_library::clock`, built with step 8's first part). The tooltip's when, and from step 12 its machine's name, from a library's `.apertrust/machines/` or, in folder mode, "another computer" |
| `source` | `StepSource` | `You`, `Auto`, `Style`, `Assistant` or `Default`: the tooltip's who and the line's tag |
| `note` | `Option<Note>` | `Note::Message`, Apertrust's own words as a message id with its arguments, worded by the client with Fluent ("Applied from IMG_2041", `history-reset`); or `Note::Text`, an assistant's or script's own words, stored as written and never translated |
| `batch` | `Option<BatchId>`, 16 hex digits | The session's, shared by the steps of one change that touched several modules, and later by those one command made on several photos; `None` for a change of one |
| `change` | `Change` | `Change::Entry`: the one module it touched with its whole stored state after it, its version, its controls set, its marks and whether it is on, as version 1 writes a module, or an unowned operation's parameters likewise while `Set` exists; or `Change::Reset`: an empty entry for each module and operation Reset reset |

- **An empty entry** is the module's reset state: its defaults, or `off` for a switched module (decision 32).
- **Planned:** the ratings, reject, labels, keywords and metadata a step sets, with the library's steps 14 and 15 (decision 47). Their encoding in a step is part of step 14's scope.
- **Room for layers** (Phase 4, [decision 34](decisions/0034-layers-masked-module-instances-in-a-fixed-order.md)): an entry's key, a module id today, gains a layer and an instance, as darktable keys its history by operation and `multi_priority`. A line then names the layer after a ·; nothing else in the step changes.

### Whole entries after, the before derived

A step stores each module's **whole state after** it, never the change alone:

- an entry migrates on its own when its module's version rises ([decision 48](decisions/0048-the-apxmp-format.md)), where a delta could not be read without the steps before it;
- the fold is a lookup of each module's last entry, with no replay;
- Compress keeps whole entries, so nothing it keeps depends on what it dropped;
- a step can be laid on another log as it is, which the session does when it takes a newer file ([Behaviour](#behaviour)).

**The before is derived, never stored:** a step's before, for a module, is that module's entry at the previous step that touched it, else its reset state. Stored, it would be a second truth beside the log. After Compress, a kept entry's before is the reset state, as darktable's compressed history shows it.

The tooltip compares the two, control by control: each control whose value or mark changed, before → after.

### What makes a step

One completed change makes a step of each module it touched: a gesture ending with `done`, a reset, a preset or a `Step`, a control's quick repeats merged into one ([below](#merging-consecutive-changes)). On the edit's own rules (decisions 28 and 42):

| Command | Step |
| --- | --- |
| `SetControl { done: true }`, with the gesture's `done: false` changes before it | One, its module's |
| `ResetControl`, `Keep`, `ApplyPreset` | One, its module's |
| `ResetModule` | One, its module's entry empty |
| `Reset` | One, with an empty entry for every module the edit holds at the end, switched modules going off (decision 32), and the note `history-reset` |
| `Step { source, note, commands }` | One for each module its commands write, in a batch where there are several |
| `Set` (unowned parameters) | None until the next change, where it is a step of its own in that change's batch |
| `CancelGesture` | None ([decision 42](decisions/0042-numbers-shown-typed-and-stored.md)) |
| `Undo`, `Redo`, `GoTo`, `Compress`, `Truncate` | None: they move the end or rewrite the log |

- **A change that changes nothing makes no step,** as it writes nothing.
- **Opening a photo with no history** makes its default steps, source Default, the session's own ([below](#original-and-the-default-steps)).
- **A pending `Set` followed by `Reset`** joins the reset's step, which resets it with the rest.
- **A pending `Set`** with no step after it becomes a step of its own, source You, before an Undo, Redo, GoTo, Compress or Truncate and as the photo closes, so no value waits outside the log.
- **Reset** (the top panel's Reset, Edit › Reset Edit, the History header's ↺) is one step that resets the edit, holding every module it reset, with the same note, on each photo of the selection when several are chosen, as a batch. The log stays; Compress shrinks it.
- **Discard History…** (decision 78; the command built, the app's menu items planned with 9.8) is darktable's discard, a command of its own, `DiscardHistory`: it clears the history and resets the edit, doing what going to Original and then Compress does, since Compress at end 0 deletes the whole log, so it makes no step, as one undo record that takes it back while the photo is open; the photo, with no history, is given its default steps again ([below](#original-and-the-default-steps)). It asks first, with Don't ask again (darktable's `ask_before_discard`). It lives in Edit › Discard History… and the History section's ⋯ options menu, never as a button in the History header nor in the photo's right-click menu.
- **The app's changes of several commands are one `Step { source: You }`:** the tone equalizer's release of the bands a drag moved, and its reset of the nine bands by a double-click or Shift+Delete, each one step, since each writes the tone equalizer alone ([modules/tone-equalizer.md](modules/tone-equalizer.md), [decision 31](decisions/0031-views-and-curves-of-the-same-stored-values.md)). A `Step` takes in the gesture under way when its commands write that gesture's module and its source and note are the gesture's, so the drag's `done: false` changes and the release are one step ([Commands](#commands)).

### A change of several modules

A step is exactly one module's change, as darktable's history item holds one module (`dt_dev_history_item_t`, `src/develop/develop.h:34`). A completed change that touches several modules, a `Step` whose commands write several, a preset row writing several, and later a style or an assistant's request, makes a step of each, as darktable's style and paste add an item per module (`src/common/styles.c`, `src/common/history.c`):

- **In the order written:** each module and operation in the order its commands first wrote it after the last `Reset`; one no command named, as a module another turned off, after them, in id order (`Change::between`).
- **One batch:** the steps share a `BatchId`, the session's, and the change's source and note, as darktable wraps the items in one undo group (`dt_undo_start_group`, `src/common/styles.c:954`; `src/common/history.c:157`, `:200`, `:229`, `:971`), so Undo and Redo move the batch whole while the History tab lists each module. A change of one module has no batch.
- **Reset is the exception:** one step noted `history-reset`, holding an empty entry for every module and operation it reset, shown as one Reset line. In a `Step`, Reset's step takes the place of the `Reset`, holding what no later command wrote, and the modules written after it follow; that step is noted `history-reset`, not with the `Step`'s note, which the other steps keep.
- **The fold is unchanged:** each module's last entry up to the end, so the batch's last step's fold is the edit the change left, and a step inside it the batch's steps up to there.

### Merging consecutive changes

A slider nudged with the arrow keys or the wheel sends a completed change each time. darktable's undo merges such changes by their target, the widget changed (`_dev_undo_start_record_target`, `src/develop/develop.c:1351-1370`), a slider's move or its reset by a double-click alike, for the widgets wired through `dt_iop_gui_changed` (`src/develop/imageop.c:3997-4006`, `src/bauhaus/bauhaus.c:1844-1857`, `:3386-3394`); a change through a module's own callback has no target and never merges (`develop.c:1359`). A change to the widget changed last starts no new undo record within `darkroom/undo/merge_same_secs` (10 s) of the record's start, nor within `darkroom/undo/review_secs` (2 s) of the pipe finishing the last change, `gui_previous_pipe_time`, set as the pipe ends (`develop.c:885`). Apertrust merges the same way, every control and preset row alike, as decision 78 decided, so one Undo takes back what one darktable undo takes back for such a widget.

A completed change merges into the step before it when all of these hold:

- both have the same **target**, darktable's widget: the control a `SetControl` or `ResetControl` names, the preset row an `ApplyPreset` names, or the picker a pick came from ([pickers.md](pickers.md#what-a-pick-writes)), of the same module; so a reset or a preset on the target changed last merges too, and a `SetControl` gesture counts as one change;
- both have the same source, You or Auto, no note and no batch, the change touches one module, and neither is a `Step` of several commands;
- the step is the log's last and the end, made by the session's previous command on the edit (a pick counting as the `Picker` command that asked for its reading, [pickers.md](pickers.md#what-a-pick-writes)), with no Undo, Redo, GoTo, Compress, Truncate or other command on the edit between;
- the change's first write came less than 10 s after the step's first, and, once the first frame whose `through` includes the step's last change is published, less than 2 s after that publication. Until that frame is published only the 10 s counts.

The merged step keeps its id, its entry takes the new state and its clock becomes the latest. A change merged adds no undo record, so one Undo takes back the merged step whole. The two times are darktable's defaults, constants rather than settings (`MERGE_WITHIN` and `REVIEW` in `session/history.rs`).

- **The times.** A change's first write is timed when the client sent its command, as the session receives each command with that time; a frame's publication when the session publishes it. A test hands both in, so it merges and splits without waiting.
- **What counts as a command between:** any command on the edit that writes no value, Undo, Redo, `GoTo` and `CancelGesture` among them, and any command refused, a `Step` refused as `DefaultSource` or `EmptyStep` too; not `RetrySave`, which changes nothing and leaves a gesture under way. A change that changes nothing, a gesture's or a `Set`'s too, makes no step and counts as none: it sets neither a target nor when the change began.
- **A `Step` of one command** has that command's target, so Auto's single writes, each a `Step { source: Auto }`, merge as the person's do. A `ResetModule`, `Keep`, `Set` or `Reset` has no target: it never merges, and a step holding one is merged into by none.
- **A merged step whose changes come back to where it began** stays, its one entry the state now, as darktable's history item stays after its undo record merged; Undo takes it back as any step.

**Pickers and Auto** write with source Auto and gesture semantics. A picker's pick is a change the session makes itself as the reading arrives, through the module's `pick` ([pickers.md](pickers.md#what-a-pick-writes), decision 79): a drag's readings are `done: false` changes ending with the release's `done: true`, one step, merged as a slider's, its target the picker. Auto sends its writes as `Step { source: Auto }`. Built with 9.5, so a pick reaches the saved log: what a picker command or a drag's picks wrote is made one step of source Auto where the session saves it, sent as `History` with the pick's values, as the drag ends, before a tool's or another drag's pick, and as the picker turns off, a slider's gesture under way a step of its own source first; the merge by picker comes with the pickers' own steps ([pickers.md](pickers.md#what-a-pick-writes)).

darktable's history items merge further: a module's consecutive changes are one item until the person focuses another module (`_dev_add_history_item_ext`). Apertrust merges steps only as darktable's undo merges changes, so the History tab and Undo always agree.

### Original and the default steps

- **Original** is end 0: no step applied, the photo before its default steps.
- **The default steps.** A photo opened with no history is given a step of source Default of each of darktable's default modules for a new raw, at its reset state, an empty entry, its defaults for the photo ([decision 21](decisions/0021-the-operation-interface.md), decision 32), as darktable's `dt_dev_read_history_ext` gives a new raw its default modules and its workflow's auto-applied presets (`src/develop/develop.c:2301-2352`). They are steps 1 to n, in darktable's history order for a new raw: those darktable has on by default with no on/off button first (Raw black/white point, Demosaic, Output colour profile), then those on by default with one (White balance, Highlight reconstruction, Orientation), as its `_dev_add_default_modules` adds them (`:2142-2171`, called at `:2339`), then those its default scene-referred workflow applies as presets after them (`_dev_auto_apply_presets`, `:2343`): Exposure, Colour calibration and the tone mapper, Apertrust's AgX; each group in the pipe's order (`history::default_modules`). Any other module, Tone curve among them, which no preset applies, has no default step and runs at its defaults. No batch: each is its own step. A client cannot send `StepSource::Default`.
- **Nothing is written as the photo opens.** A log holding the default steps alone is not written, wherever its end, so moving among them before the first change writes nothing; the first change writes them with it, and a photo left untouched keeps no file. Once the log is in the file, a move among them is saved as any move of the end.
- **Stable numbers.** A history that has steps is given none: its steps keep their numbers, and a default module a later build adds runs at its defaults with no step. So is an edit read from version 1, a step of each module it holds and no default step, and any history with steps but no default steps: darktable applies its workflow's auto-applied presets once in a history's lifetime (`DT_IMAGE_AUTO_PRESETS_APPLIED`, `:1866-1871`), and one rule keeps every history's numbers. Such a photo's Original shows every module at its defaults, none left out. A photo whose history is discarded has none, and is given its default steps again, as darktable clears its flag with the history (`src/common/history.c:54`).
- **Undo stops above them.** Undo's fallback never moves the end over a default step, as darktable's auto-applied items are no undo records; Undo still takes back a record that moved the end among them, a `GoTo`.
- **The end among them** (`GoTo`, or Undo and Redo of one) shows the photo with the first k default steps applied. A module whose default step lies after the end, and that darktable leaves off until its workflow applies it, Exposure, Colour calibration and the tone mapper, is not applied: the edit holds it as not applied, `edit::instances` leaves out each operation it owns a parameter of, and `ModuleState::enabled` reports it off, as darktable's photo below its auto-applied items shows. The modules darktable has on by default run at their defaults below their steps, as darktable's `dt_dev_pop_history_items_ext` resets each module to `default_enabled` (`:1614-1636`), so Original renders as the end after Orientation's step. It is the history's state at that step, never an edit turning a module off (decision 32); every reader of the file folds the modules not applied from the log at its saved end (`sidecar`'s `Loaded::edit`, the log's fold), the file-based export, `apertrust-cli export` and `preview` and the thumbnails among them, so they render what the view shows; the live export and the open photo's thumbnail render the edit as the file holds it (`Open::saved_edit`), every module applied while the log of default steps alone is not yet written.
- **The first change with the end among them** keeps every default step after the end, moved below it before the change's steps, and applies them all; its Undo puts the default steps back after the end. A gesture's first change applies them already, and a gesture whose release leaves the values as they were leaves them not applied again. Reset and a module's reset there move no value but are one step each, a Reset holding an empty entry for each module not applied, a module's reset that module's, so the edit is the photo's defaults with every module applied. A module's picker turned on there is the first change too: a step of its module, saved, which applies them, its operation then planned and its module on, as darktable's picker turning its module on is a history item. Reset Edit and the History header's ↺ are available there though the edit holds nothing, since Reset applies them. An override `apertrust-cli` applies to a saved edit whose end lies among them, `export --set`, `--param` or `preview --exposure`, is a change too: it applies them, so it renders what `edit` with the same flags, then `export`, would.
- **Unlike darktable, on purpose.** darktable's `_dev_add_history_item_ext` keeps above the end only an always-on module's item with no earlier entry, moved below it (`:1191-1229`), and drops the auto-applied items of Exposure, Colour calibration and the tone mapper, which are not on by default and have an on/off button (`:1215-1222`), so its photo goes on without them. Apertrust keeps every default step, the modules every photo runs never being off (decision 32). darktable also keeps a person's later item of an always-on module with no earlier entry, where Apertrust keeps only the default steps.
- **Planned:** darktable's auto-applied presets (Phase 3) and a project's start adjustments (the library's step 38) are written as default steps too, by the session itself as they are applied.

## Behaviour

**Undo follows darktable's undo** (`src/libs/history.c:664-686`: every change to the history, a click on an item among them, records the history and its end for Ctrl+Z). The session keeps, in memory, a stack of **undo records**, each what one command on the history changed:

| Record | Undo | Redo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One change's steps added, one or a batch's | The steps leave the log, as darktable's undo restores the history without them, a batch's whole as darktable's undo group; any steps they dropped after the end come back, and the end as it was | The steps again, the steps after the end dropped again |
| `GoTo` | The end where it was | The end where `GoTo` put it |
| `Compress` | The log as it was before | Compress again |
| `Truncate` | The log as it was before | Truncate again |
| `DiscardHistory` | The log as it was before | Discard again, the default steps given anew |

A change merged into the step before it adds no record, as darktable's merged change records no undo: Undo takes back the merged step whole.

- **Two stacks:** the done records and the undone ones. Undo takes the newest done record back and puts it on the undone stack; Redo puts the newest undone record back and returns it to the done stack; a new command on the history clears the undone stack. Both are the session's memory alone, not a second log: they end as the photo closes, as darktable's end on leaving the darkroom.
- **The fallback.** Only when the done stack is empty, as after reopening a photo, does Undo move the end back through the steps the sidecar kept, over the step before it and the rest of its batch before that, never over a default step; only when the undone stack is empty does Redo move it forward over the next step and the rest of its batch. Each fallback move is recorded on the opposite stack, Undo's on the undone stack and Redo's on the done stack, so the next Redo or Undo reverses it, and is named as `Move::Steps` of the steps it crosses, not as a `GoTo`; a fallback move clears no undone record. With nothing to undo or redo they change nothing; the `History` fact says beforehand what they would do (`undo`, `redo`).
- **Each move of the end,** by Undo, Redo or `GoTo`, holds the edit it moves to to the ranges its modules' values give on the photo, as the photo's open does, a value clamped a change not yet a step (decision 30). Such a clamp, a move's or the open's, never becomes a step of its own: where it is the only change not yet a step, the next move, and the photo's close, leave it as it is rather than make it one, so Undo and Redo pass an out-of-range step both ways.
- **Going to a step** (`GoTo`) sets the end just after it, any step, one inside a batch among them, as darktable's click on any item, or to 0 for Original. The later steps stay, greyed, and are saved, so Redo's fallback still reaches them after reopening. Undo takes the click back, as darktable's does (`src/libs/history.c:1297-1318`, recorded by `:664-686`), and after a click and an edit, which drops the later steps, Undo brings them back while the photo is open. Going to where the end is already changes nothing and records nothing, as darktable's click on the item applied last does nothing (`src/libs/history.c:1265`).
- **An edit while the end is below the last step** drops the steps after the end, then adds its own, as darktable's `_dev_add_history_item_ext` removes the items above `history_end` (`src/develop/develop.c:1191-1229`). darktable keeps one kind above the end, moving it below: an always-on module's item (default-enabled, or with no on-off button) with no earlier entry, its first item of a new photo. Apertrust keeps every default step after the end, and only those ([above](#original-and-the-default-steps)). Until the photo closes, Undo brings the dropped steps back; after that they are gone.
- **Reset** is a step (above): Undo brings the edit back.
- **Compress** is darktable's compress history stack (`src/libs/history.c:1239-1242`, `dt_history_compress_on_image` in `src/common/history.c:1193`). darktable keeps, of the items below `history_end`, the last of each operation and instance and deletes every other item, those above the end among them (`src/common/history.c:1244-1255`), and at end 0 deletes the whole history (`:1212-1216`). So Apertrust's Compress keeps **every default step**, wherever the end is; among the person's steps up to the end, each module's and field's last step, with its id, clock, source, note and batch, dropping one whose entry is empty, Reset's among them, since the reset state is what no entry gives; drops the person's steps after the end; and makes no step. The end is then the last step kept up to it; where it lies among the default steps, those after it stay after it. At end 0 it drops every step, as darktable deletes the whole history.
- **Truncate** is darktable's Ctrl-click on the same button (`src/libs/history.c:1244-1250`, `dt_history_truncate_on_image` in `src/common/history.c:1348-1399`): it deletes the items at and above `history_end` and nothing else (`:1366-1376`), all of them at end 0 (`:1357-1362`). So Apertrust's Truncate drops the person's steps after the end, keeping every step up to it as it is and the default steps after it, and makes no step; at end 0 it drops every step.
- **Neither changes what the photo shows,** at the end, at Original or at a default step, as darktable keeps its auto-applied flag so neither changes its photo: the default steps are what Original and the Defaults group show, and those after the end leave their modules not applied. Only at end 0 do they change it, back to a new photo's default look. Keeping a default step a later step of its module follows differs from darktable, whose compress needs no item for a module at its default, so the Defaults group stays whole.
- **A log left with no step,** by Discard History, or by Compress or Truncate at end 0, is a photo with no history: it is given its default steps again, the end after them, as a new photo is. The file is written with no step, so it holds nothing of the history it had, and the default steps go into it with the first change; reopened before then, the photo is given them again.
- **One that changes nothing,** a Compress of a log compressed already, a Truncate with no step after the end, a Discard History of default steps never written with the end after them, records nothing and writes nothing, as `GoTo` to the end does.
- **Both are undoable:** darktable's `_lib_history_truncate` (`src/libs/history.c:1189-1235`) records an undo for each, and Undo puts the log back while the photo is open.
- **The clock.** Each step carries its own clock, and each move of the end (Undo, Redo, GoTo) and each Compress or Truncate, or its Undo or Redo, advances the copy's clock vector with a clock of its own, so two copies of the sidecar converge under [decision 52](decisions/0052-comparing-two-copies-of-a-sidecar.md).
- **A gesture under way** is ended as completed by Undo, Redo, GoTo, Compress and Truncate, as by any command on the edit (decision 42), so Undo during a drag takes back the drag.
- **A batch on several photos:** Undo on a batch takes it back on every photo where its last step is still the end, whichever of them is active; a photo changed since keeps it under its own steps, and the notice says so ([gui.md](gui.md#selection-and-the-active-photo), decision 37). After a restart the library's index finds the batch's photos by the batch id of each photo's last applied step. Planned with several photos in one session; until then a batch has one photo.
- **Leaving and reopening a photo:** the log and its end are in the sidecar, so Undo and Redo come back through their fallback, and so do the greyed steps; the undo records do not.
- **A crash** loses at most the gesture in flight: each completed change, Undo, Redo, GoTo, Compress and Truncate is handed to the edit writer at once (decision 47).
- **A newer file on disk.** Before every write, a cancel's held write among them, the writer compares the file's size and modified time with those the session last read or it last wrote, the written file's taken from the file itself after its rename, so another program's write just after it is never taken for it. Where they differ, as after `apertrust-cli edit` wrote a step, it writes nothing and reads the newer file for the session (`Engine::newer_file`). Every write handed over before the session takes the file is dropped unwritten (the writer's generation, raised as a file is taken and as another photo opens). The session compares the file with its **base**, what the file held as the session last read it or the writer last wrote it, its steps by id and clock and its clock vector, never with the session's own vector, which this machine's other programs share (decision 52):
  - **Nothing newer:** every machine's clock in the file's vector at or below the base's, as an older copy put back, or no file at all. The session's log is written over it, with nothing to tell.
  - **Newer:** the session lays the steps up to its end that neither the newer log nor the base holds, by id and clock, the steps not yet saved, on it after its end, as an edit after Undo would, a step whose id the newer log holds already taking a new one, and a step the other side dropped staying dropped; keeps a change not yet a step, a gesture under way among them, on top; joins the clock vectors; writes the joined log; and sends `Values` and `History` again, and a frame where the edit changed. A step laid holds its module's whole state, so where both sides changed one module the session's state is the module's at the end, the other side's change kept in the log beneath it; a merge module by module is decision 52's later setting. The undo records and the merge state are cleared, since they describe a log that is gone, so Undo moves the end. A Compress, Truncate or Discard History not yet saved that took out steps the base holds is not kept: the newer log keeps them, its end stands, and `SessionError::MoveNotKept` says so.
  - **One this build cannot write over:** a newer Apertrust's, or one with a `sidecar::Problem`. The photo shows it as it reads, for viewing only: `Opened` comes again with the same open's number and its `view_only`, so the caption says why; the session's steps not yet saved are not written, and `SessionError::NotSaved` says so; a module's picker turns off; and every change is refused, a pick's too.
  - **One that cannot be read,** as a sync tool's half-written file: each write it drops answered with `SessionError::NotSaved`, with the commands it includes, which the app takes as that write's failure, so no write stays awaited, the log saying it once a file; and never written over, the writer keeping the stamp it had, so each later write finds it again and writes nothing, until it reads and is taken in. The session's steps stay in memory meanwhile, to be laid on it, and the photo's close hands over a write that finds it again: once it reads, they reach it; while it does not, the close tells it, as the photo's last word, which `Open` and `Close` pass on. This rather than view-only, which would never write them.
  - **A move not yet saved,** an Undo, Redo or `GoTo` with no step to lay, is made again on the newer log where the other side only added steps after the base's end: the end goes back to the step the session's end is on, the steps added after it, so an Undo stays undone. Where the other side moved its end, a Redo of a step of the base among its moves, or dropped steps of the base, or the session's end is on a step the newer log lacks, the newer end stands, unless it is on the session's end's step already, and `SessionError::MoveNotKept` says so, a lasting notice in the app (`edit-move-not-kept`). An Undo that took steps the base applied out of the session's log does not take them out of the newer one: with no step to lay they come after the end, where Redo's fallback reaches them; with an edit after that Undo, its steps laid on the newer log, they apply again beneath them, and `MoveNotKept` says so too, as it does for a step of the base the session applies and the base did not, by a Redo before a change, that the joined log does not; a step the base applied already that the other side dropped is the other side's change, and tells nothing, as does one the base had not applied, dropped by the session's edit and redone by the other side.
  - **What other tools wrote** beside the edit since, as a rating, is kept when the file holds nothing newer of the history, as when it is taken in.
  - **As the photo closes,** its last write is waited for, so a newer file it finds is taken in and its log written, and waited for too, before the next photo opens, as that open's read of its own sidecar waits for the writes before it anyway; nothing is rendered then. A file another program changes again between the take-in and its write is taken in again, twice more; still changing, it is told as `NotSaved` (`SessionError::StillChanging`), the close's steps not written.
  - **Steps laid,** or a move made again, on a newer log get a clock of their own in the copy's vector, as a move of the end does, so the file written is newer than the one read for every reader, this machine's other programs among them.
  - **Where the file holds nothing newer** of the first copy's history, the session's log is written into the file as read: other tools' properties and the other virtual copies, whatever another program changed of them, kept.
  - **A known limit** (decision 52): the check narrows the window to the rename that replaces the file, which no filesystem Apertrust supports makes conditional on the file it replaces. Another program's write that lands between the check and the rename is lost, as with any tool that writes the same file without a lock; the CLI's `edit`, which checks its file the same way, has the same limit.
  - This is the part of the library's step 10 (read before every write, decision 52) that history needs; step 10 adds the rest: a diverged copy, both sides changed, kept as a virtual copy of its own rather than laid on the newer log.
- **A photo open for viewing only, or not yet live** ([decision 77](decisions/0077-a-photo-opens-in-stages.md)): its history is sent and shown; Undo, Redo, GoTo, Compress, Truncate and `Step` are commands on the edit, refused as every change is (`ViewOnly`, `NotLive`). `DiscardHistory` is the one exception, and only for an edit view-only because of a `sidecar::Problem`, any of them, never for one a newer build wrote: warning first, so the person can keep a copy of the file, it writes a fresh log, and what the file held that could not be read, part of the history, a field a save would drop, another virtual copy's, goes with the old log. The fresh log is a new photo's default steps, then a step of each module and operation the edit holds as far as it was read, sharing a batch, source You, noted `history-discard`, the end the last of them; the default steps of the modules the edit as read holds not applied come after the end, so they stay so and nothing changes on screen, and Original is the untouched photo. It ends the view-only state: `Opened` comes again with the same open's number and no `view_only`, then its values, editable, and its history. It makes no undo record, as the log it replaces cannot be written back. Default steps alone are written as no step, as a new photo's are (decision 78).
- **A sidecar changed on another computer:** until the library's step 12, step 10 opens a diverged sidecar view-only; step 12 keeps the other side as a virtual copy with its own log (decision 52). Joining two logs step by step comes with step 14.

## Through the session

### Commands

All but `Hide` are commands on the edit (`on_edit`): each ends a gesture under way, a gesture being one module's changes of one source and note, but for a change that goes on with it or takes it in, of its source and note, and a `Step` refused as `DefaultSource` or `EmptyStep`, which leaves it under way; each is followed by a `Values`, and is refused on a photo open for viewing only or not yet live.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Undo` | Takes back the newest done record, a batch's steps whole; only when there is none, moves the end back over the step before it and its batch |
| `Redo` | Puts back the newest undone record; only when there is none, moves the end forward over the next step and its batch |
| `GoTo { step: Option<StepId> }` | The end just after `step`, one inside a batch among them, or Original with `None`, once a change not yet a step is made one; a step not in the log then refused, `UnknownStep` |
| `Compress` | Compress History, as above, darktable's compress |
| `Truncate` | The steps after the end dropped, as above, darktable's Ctrl-click on compress |
| `Reset` | The whole edit back to nothing, as one step noted `history-reset`, holding every module it reset |
| `DiscardHistory` | Discard History…, darktable's discard, the log cleared and the edit reset, as going to Original and then Compress, one undo record; on an edit view-only because of a `sidecar::Problem`, the one command allowed, a fresh log of a new photo's default steps and a step of each module the edit holds as far as it was read, sharing a batch, noted `history-discard` |
| `Step { source, note, commands }` | Several commands on the edit as one change with that source and note, a step of each module they write, in a batch where there are several |
| `Hide { module, hidden }` | The eye held: the live frame shown as if the module were reset, and let go, as the edit has it; no step, nothing written |

**`Step`** is how a client names a source and a note:

- it holds only `SetControl`, `ResetControl`, `ResetModule`, `Keep`, `ApplyPreset`, `Set` and `Reset`;
- they are applied in order to the edit in place, all or none: the edit, its held coordinates and whether the writer has it are kept aside and put back should one be refused, so one refused refuses the whole and changes nothing: `SessionError::Refused { command, error: InStep { index, error } }`, naming the `Step`'s number, the failing command's index, counting from 0, and its error;
- any other command inside, a `Step` among them, is refused the same way at its index, `NotInStep`;
- `source` cannot be `StepSource::Default`, which the session alone writes: refused, `DefaultSource`; a `Step` holding no command is refused, `EmptyStep`; both before the gesture under way is touched, so it goes on;
- it completes as one change when it holds any command but `SetControl { done: false }`, a step of each module it writes, sharing a batch id, which one Undo takes back; one of `done: false` changes alone is a gesture's change of its source and note, on the module of the last of them;
- **one source per gesture:** a gesture has the source and note of the change that began it. A `done: false` change on its module of another source or note ends it as a step of its own and begins another with its own; a completed change takes it in, a release on its module or a `Step` writing it, only with its source and note, and otherwise the gesture is a step of its own first. A `Step` refused begins no gesture, and one that would have taken the gesture in leaves it a step of its own, as a release refused does;
- a client's plain commands are steps of source You with no note;
- the app sends Auto's writes as `Step { source: Auto }` (a picker's are the session's own, [pickers.md](pickers.md#what-a-pick-writes)), a style's as `Step { source: Style, note }` (Phase 3), and its own changes of several commands as `Step { source: You }`; the MCP adapter sends one `Step { source: Assistant, note }` per request (Phase 6).

**A batch id is the session's,** never a client's: one change of several modules gets one `BatchId`, written into each of its steps, and one command acting on several photos will get one, written into each photo's steps. A client cannot make two requests one batch, nor two clients collide on an id.

**`Hide { module, hidden }`** (the eye) is view state, as `Resize` and `Scopes` are. The app sends it with `hidden` true as the eye is pressed and false as it is let go: a peek, never a toggle. While hidden, the live frame renders as if the module were reset: off for a switched module, its defaults for one every photo runs, so the eye on an untouched module every photo runs changes nothing. A module every photo runs keeps its operation, at its defaults: skipping it, for White balance or the tone mapper, would show an image no edit can make; a switched module, off, drops out of the plan as any switched module off does. It is no step, writes nothing, changes no export, still or thumbnail, and ends as the photo closes or another opens. Hiding never adds an effect: a default module the history's end leaves not applied stays out of the plan when hidden. `Values` reports it as `ModuleState::hidden`. A module's picker reads the view and writes the edit, so while any module is hidden one already on measures nothing and applies no reading, its reading due waiting until all are shown, when the next reading applies as usual, and `Picker` `On` for a module's picker is refused, `SessionError::Hidden`; the Colour picker module's picker, which reads the frame shown and writes nothing, works as before. A layer's eye comes with layers.

### The `History` event

`Event::History(History)`, a fact (`Fact::History`), the whole topic each time ([decision 59](decisions/0059-one-event-system.md)):

```rust
pub struct History {
    /// How many commands it includes, as `Values::through` counts them.
    pub through: u64,
    /// Oldest first; a client lists them newest first. Each behind an `Arc`, so
    /// facts sent one after another share the steps they have in common.
    pub steps: Vec<Arc<HistoryStep>>,
    /// How many steps apply, darktable's `history_end`: 0 is Original, and the
    /// step numbered `end`, counting from 1, is the last applied.
    pub end: usize,
    /// What Undo and Redo would do now, so a menu names it; `None` when nothing.
    pub undo: Option<Move>,
    pub redo: Option<Move>,
}

pub struct HistoryStep {
    pub id: StepId,
    pub clock: Clock,
    pub source: StepSource,
    pub note: Option<Note>,
    /// Shared by the steps of one change of several modules, which Undo and
    /// Redo move whole.
    pub batch: Option<BatchId>,
    /// The module the step touched, with what changed: one, or for a reset
    /// each module it reset.
    pub changes: Vec<ModuleChange>,
}

pub struct ModuleChange {
    /// Borrowed for a module or operation this build knows, owned for one it does not.
    pub module: Cow<'static, str>,
    /// Before and after, where the step turned the module on or off.
    pub enabled: Option<[bool; 2]>,
    pub controls: Vec<ControlChange>,
}

pub struct ControlChange {
    pub control: &'static str,
    pub before: ControlValue,
    pub after: ControlValue,
    pub mark: [Option<Mark>; 2],
}

pub enum Move {
    /// One change's steps, a batch's whole, added, taken back or put back, or
    /// the end moved over them; a batch's part where `GoTo` left the end
    /// inside it.
    Steps { first: StepId, count: usize },
    /// A `GoTo` taken back or put back.
    GoTo,
    /// A `Compress`, `Truncate` or `DiscardHistory` taken back or put back.
    Compress,
    Truncate,
    DiscardHistory,
}
```

- **The types** live in `apertrust_engine::history`, re-exported by the session, plain data that moves into `apertrust-protocol` unchanged when it is made. `Clock` and `MachineId` are `apertrust_library::clock`'s, built with step 8's first part. `StepSource`, `StepId`, `BatchId` and `Note` are written by name or as text on the wire, a client ignoring a source it does not know.
- **When it is sent:** at a photo's open, right after its `Values { live: false }` (decision 77's stage 2); in each batch whose commands changed the log, its end or the undo records, once, after the batch's `Values` and before its `Frame`; and when the session takes a newer file from disk. A gesture's `done: false` changes change no step and send none.
- **Whole, not a delta.** A fact is always its whole topic (decision 59), a remote client is sent the latest and a client joining late needs nothing more. It is sent once per completed change, never per frame of a drag, and in process the steps it shares with the last are not copied. A step with one control changed is about 200 bytes in memory and 160 as JSON, so 200 steps make about 40 kB and 1,000 about 200 kB, estimated, a curve's points more. Deltas would need ordered notices, `Sync` replaying them and a merge in every client, for a topic smaller than one frame.
- **Its budgets:** at 200 and at 1,000 steps on the M4, building the fact takes under 1 ms, and its JSON, as the remote protocol will encode it, is under 256 kB. A measurement that misses its budget brings the planned fallback: the fact holds the step range the client shows, which it names with a command, the count and the end always whole, so a client still knows where the end is and what Undo would do.
- **Measured in 9.1** on the M4, release build, each step one control changed, three runs alike (`the_fact_s_budgets` in `session/history/tests.rs`, ignored, run by hand):

  | Steps | A batch's fact | Every step described anew | JSON, serde's derive | Its `null`s left out |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | 200 | 0.007 ms | 0.08 ms | 59 kB | 51 kB |
  | 1,000 | 0.014 ms | 0.35 to 0.40 ms | 295 kB | 255 kB |

  A batch describes only the steps it added, the rest shared behind their `Arc`s, so the time is well within its budget. The JSON is an estimate, the test's imitation of serde's derive, nothing in process serialising the fact: a step is about 295 bytes, not the 160 estimated, its id, clock and source as text, its note, batch and `enabled` as `null`, and both values and marks named.
- **So the fallback is planned with the remote protocol,** the fact's only reader that serialises it: 295 kB at 1,000 steps, and 255 kB with its `null`s left out, miss the budget, so there the fact holds the step range a client names, with the count, the end, `undo` and `redo` always whole. Leaving out a field that is `None` (serde's `skip_serializing_if`) is a wire convention of `apertrust-protocol`; field names are not shortened.
- **The app's mirror** keeps the latest, by its `through`, and drops a replaced open's as it drops its values (`PhotoState::stale`).

**Gaps left for later, on purpose:**

- `History` names no photo or copy: it is the active photo's, its open's number ordering it. With several photos (decision 37) and virtual copies (step 11) it gains the photo's `PhotoRef`, its copy included.
- `Step` names no photos: with several photos it gains them, a batch's.
- `ModuleChange` is keyed by module alone: layers (Phase 4) add a layer and an instance to its key.
- An unowned operation's parameters, while `Set` exists, show as a `ModuleChange` under the operation's id, its controls the parameters, an integer as a number.
- **Built:** `Move::Steps` and `Move::GoTo`, and with 9.8's engine half `Move::Compress`, `Move::Truncate` and `Move::DiscardHistory`. A move of the end by Undo's or Redo's fallback names the steps it takes back or puts back, as `Move::Steps`.
- **Built (9.6):** a module or an operation this build does not know, as in a photo a newer build wrote, open for viewing only, is a `ModuleChange` under its stored name, owned, with no control, since nothing here can describe its values; the History tab lists the step by that name.

## In the sidecar

History is part of `.apxmp` version 2 (decision 47; [library.md](library.md#version-2)), written through the XMP layer ([decision 50](decisions/0050-our-own-xmp-layer.md)) in one fixed layout, read and written back byte for byte. **Its target is conformance to ISO 16684-1:2019 (which revises ISO 16684-1:2012, whose text Adobe publishes free as the XMP Specification Part 1),** XMP Part 1: data model, serialization and core properties, as the XMP layer's is, held by the layer's fixtures and by 8.2's fixture below: every property is in a namespace declared with its URI, `apertrust`, `control` and `param` among them (decision 48), arrays are `rdf:Seq` and `rdf:Bag`, structures are nested `rdf:Description`s, and times are XMP Date values, so ExifTool, exiv2 and other XMP readers read the whole file.

Built with step 8.2, the text layer's `sidecar::Edits` (`crates/library/src/sidecar/copies.rs`). **The file** holds `apertrust:version` 2, `apertrust:photo`, the photo's file name, `apertrust:fingerprint`, its size, a colon and 64 hex digits ([library.md](library.md#fingerprints)), and `apertrust:copies`, an `rdf:Seq` of its virtual copies. It declares a fourth namespace, `https://apertrust.org/ns/xmp/arg/` (`arg`), for notes' arguments ([decision 48](decisions/0048-the-apxmp-format.md)).

**Each virtual copy** holds `apertrust:id`, 8 hex digits, `apertrust:number`, `apertrust:name` (empty for none), `apertrust:end`, the id of the last step applied (empty for Original), its clock vector, `apertrust:clocks`, an `rdf:Bag` of one clock per machine, written in the order of their machine ids so two machines writing the same copy write the same bytes (decision 52), and `apertrust:history`, an `rdf:Seq` of its steps, oldest first. An empty array is closed at once (`<rdf:Bag/>`), as ExifTool reads the whitespace inside an open one as text. Each step holds, in this order:

- `apertrust:id`, 16 hex digits;
- its clock in three fields: `apertrust:when`, the time as an XMP Date in ISO 8601 with milliseconds in UTC (`2026-03-14T14:02:11.120Z`), so other tools read it as a date; `apertrust:counter`, the hybrid logical clock's counter; and `apertrust:machine`, the machine id. A clock in the copy's vector is the same three fields;
- `apertrust:source` by name: `you`, `auto`, `style`, `assistant` or `default`;
- its note: `apertrust:note`, the words as written, or `apertrust:noteMessage`, the message id;
- `apertrust:batch`, 16 hex digits, when it has one;
- a message's arguments, where it has any: `apertrust:noteArgs`, a struct whose fields are the arguments in the `arg` namespace by their names (`<apertrust:noteArgs><rdf:Description arg:style="Film grain"/></apertrust:noteArgs>`). Nested, an argument named as a step's own field, `id` or `when`, stays apart from it in tools that name a struct's fields by their local names alone, as ExifTool does;
- `apertrust:entries`, one `rdf:Seq` of its modules' and unowned operations' entries in the step's order, each written as version 1 writes it.

Every value is read as the text the file holds but the clocks. What cannot be read as written is read as far as it can be and told to the caller as a `sidecar::Problem` in `Edits::problems`, as decision 48 leaves out or clamps an unreadable value with a warning and the photo opens: a step's clock that cannot be read keeps the step with no clock; a clock of the vector that cannot be read is left out; a step with both `apertrust:note` and `apertrust:noteMessage` reads the message; either that is no plain text (a struct, an array, a resource or a text with qualifiers) is left out; `apertrust:noteArgs` without `apertrust:noteMessage` is left out, and so is whatever `apertrust:noteArgs` holds that is not a text argument (a field of another namespace, an argument that is no plain text, `noteArgs` that is no struct). So is what the engine reads as a number or a name, kept as written: a step's id or batch that is not 16 lowercase hex digits, a source none of the five (`sidecar::SOURCES`), and an end that names none of the copy's steps, every step then taken as applied; a step's id twice in its copy (`DuplicateStep`); a step's clock past its machine's clock in the copy's vector, or of a machine the vector lacks (`ClockPastVector`), as the vector is to hold the latest clock of each machine that changed the copy; `apertrust:entries` that is no `rdf:Seq`; and a field a save would drop (below). An edit with any problem opens for viewing only (`sidecar::Reason::Unreadable`), so nothing is written over what this build could not read; its caption says some of its history can't be read, not that a newer build wrote it. A file of a version above 2 is read as version 2 lays it out, and `Edits::newer` names it. A whole file with one step:

- **A fixture holds it:** `crates/library/tests/fixtures/xmp/version-2-history.apxmp`, one virtual copy with five steps, the end on the fourth, every source but Default, a note in words, a message with its arguments, one of them named `id`, a batch, a switched module on, an unowned operation's entry and two machines' clocks. - **The end is stored as a step's id,** not a count, so it stays right when two logs join by clock (step 14); the protocol's `end` is its count, worked out as the log is read.
- **Version 1 reads as a step of each module,** modules first, then each operation's unowned parameters, each in id order, whatever the file's order: source You, no note, sharing a batch where there are several, so one Undo takes the edit read back whole; the first step's id the first 16 hex digits of the BLAKE3 hash of the file's bytes, each next one more, and the batch's the next 16, so every machine reading the same file gives the same steps; their clock's time the file's modified time, its counter 0 and its machine id `00000000`, unknown; the end after the last; a file with no module and no parameter reads as no step, end 0. Only such a file, or none, is a photo with no history given its default steps ([Original and the default steps](#original-and-the-default-steps)); every other log, an older build's version 2 among them, is given none. Version 0 reads through version 1's reading, as now. Such a file is written as version 2 only at the next change. `Edits::parse` is the one conversion, the engine's `sidecar` reading each step's entries with the module definitions as for a file of version 2. `Edits::parse` reads it as one virtual copy, `sidecar::FIRST_COPY`, id `00000000`, number 0 and no name, the same on every machine so that two machines changing the same version 1 file compare one copy (decision 52), its history those steps, its end the last step's id and its clock vector their clock; a file holding no entry reads as the copy with no step, its end empty.
- **Every entry of every step** follows decision 48's rules, not only the edit at the end, since a save writes them all: a newer module version or an unknown module anywhere in the log makes the photo view-only, and an older one is migrated entry by entry. An entry this build leaves out with a warning, a retired module's or an operation's it does not run unedited, is left out of its step, and a step left with none is left out, its place in the end with it.
- **The engine reads the first copy,** the one a photo opens, and writes its log, end and clock vector into the file as read: the photo's name and fingerprint, worked out as the photo opens, and any other copy kept as it was, so a save drops nothing (several copies are the library's step 11).
- **Compress and Truncate in the file** leave only the steps they kept, and the copy's clock vector advanced with their own clock; Discard History, and either at end 0, leave the copy with no step and its end empty, the vector advanced likewise. Discard History on an edit whose history cannot be read writes its fresh log there, the copy's other fields and the other copies kept as read.
- **No limit on the number of steps,** as darktable has none; Compress and Truncate are how the person shrinks a log. As written, one module's step of two controls set and kept is about 720 bytes, so 1,000 steps are about 0.7 MB (decision 47). A 1,000-step log is read within 20 ms and written within 50 ms on the M4's SSD, measured below; over SMB to the NAS it is still to be measured.
- **What other tools write beside the edit,** at the file's top level in their own namespaces, as ExifTool's `dc:subject` or `xmp:Rating`, is kept as read (`Edits::other`) and written back after Apertrust's own properties. A property of Apertrust's own at the top that this build does not know, any field in a copy, a step or a step's entry that it does not write, of any namespace, and an entries item that is no entry, are a `Problem` (`UnknownField`), so the edit opens for viewing only rather than lose them, its caption saying some of the history can't be kept (decision 48).
- **Measured with 9.5** on the M4's SSD, release build, a log of 1,000 steps of Exposure, two controls set and kept in each, each figure the median of nine runs (`a_1000_step_log_reads_within_20_ms_and_writes_within_50_ms` in the engine's `sidecar`, ignored, run by hand; it fails over either bound), four runs with the machine otherwise quiet and three while other builds loaded it:

### Step 8 first, in two parts

History needs two parts of the library's step 8, and they come first:

- **8.1, the clock and the machine id,** built in `apertrust-library`'s `clock`, before the log, so every step carries its real clock from its first build. The system's machine id is read through the safe crate `machine-uid`, with no `unsafe` of Apertrust's ([library.md](library.md#version-2)).
- **8.2, version 2's text layer,** built in `apertrust-library`'s `sidecar` before history is saved: the photo's identity (`apertrust:photo` and `apertrust:fingerprint`), and `apertrust:copies` with its virtual copies, each with its id, number, name, clock vector, log and end, each time an XMP Date; version 1 read as a step of each module, sharing a batch; the history fixture ExifTool and exiv2 read; and the clocks' drift bound, `Clock::observe` ignoring another machine's clock read more than 5 minutes past the wall clock ([decision 47](decisions/0047-where-edits-live-and-what-the-sidecar-holds.md)).

**Why history does not land in a version 2 of its own first:** history belongs inside `apertrust:copies`, one log per virtual copy. Holding it elsewhere first and moving it into `apertrust:copies` with step 8 would change how existing version 2 files read, against decision 48's one fixed layout per version: a third version, and builds between the two layouts, nightly ones among them, opening each other's edits view-only.

**What of step 8 remains for later:** machines' names in a library's `.apertrust/machines/` (Phase 5); the fingerprint's uses, re-pairing a renamed raw (step 18) and keying thumbnails (step 6); the commands for more than one virtual copy, and tombstones (step 11); comparing two copies by their clock vectors (steps 10 and 12).

## The CLI

`apertrust-cli` works on the sidecar directly through the engine's `history` and `sidecar`, as `edit` does today, never through a session. The app's session finds such a write before its own and takes the newer file ([Behaviour](#behaviour)).

- **`edit FILE`** adds one change for all its flags, source You, with `--note TEXT` for its note: a step of each module its flags write, in the order of its flags, sharing a batch, as the session's change of several modules is. `--clear` resets the edit within that change, the log kept, as one Reset step first. An edit while the end is below the last step drops the later steps, keeping the default steps, as in the app; a photo with no history is given no default step by the CLI, which writes its change alone (the session gives them as the photo opens); one that changes nothing writes nothing. Built with 9.5 but `--note`, which comes with 9.9; the file is written as version 2, and an edit open for viewing only is refused.
- **`history FILE`** (planned) lists the steps newest first, the end marked, each with its number, source, note, time and changes, before → after; `--undo [N]` and `--redo [N]` move the end, `--to STEP|original` goes to a step, `--compress` compresses, `--truncate` drops the steps after the end and `--discard` discards the history as `DiscardHistory` does, an edit view-only because of a `sidecar::Problem` included. Each move advances the copy's clock vector, as the session's do. The CLI keeps no undo records, so its Undo and Redo move the end. An edit a newer Apertrust wrote is refused, as `edit` refuses it.
